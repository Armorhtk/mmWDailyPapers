## UAV 天底方向的 3D 雷达成像

原英文标题：3D Radar Imaging from the UAV Nadir

作者：S. Hamed Javadi, Hichem Sahli, André Bourdoux

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-04

原英文摘要：Radars improve the sensing robustness of UAVs by operating under poor lighting and weather conditions and seeing through occlusions such as vegetation. However, they suffer from poor angular resolution, which can be addressed using synthetic aperture radar (SAR) algorithms. State-of-the-art UAV SAR methods operate at a depression angle and are not suitable for sensor fusion applications where the data are collected from areas directly below the UAV (i.e., the UAV nadir). In this paper, we present an interferometric SAR (InSAR) framework for reconstructing 3D images from the UAV nadir using a low-cost multi-input-multi-output (MIMO) mm-wave radar. Additionally, an effective method based on the phase gradient autofocus (PGA) is presented for compensating the phase error across the virtual receive antennas. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our 3D imaging algorithm in both simulation and experimental scenarios.

中文摘要：雷达通过在恶劣光照和天气条件下运行以及穿透植被等遮挡物进行观测，提高了无人机的感知鲁棒性。然而，它们存在角分辨率差的问题，这可以通过合成孔径雷达（SAR）算法来解决。最先进的无人机 SAR 方法工作在俯角下，不适用于数据从无人机正下方区域（即无人机天底）采集的传感器融合应用。在本文中，我们提出了一种干涉合成孔径雷达（InSAR）框架，用于使用低成本多输入多输出（MIMO）毫米波雷达从无人机天底重建 3D 图像。此外，提出了一种基于相位梯度自聚焦（PGA）的有效方法，用于补偿跨虚拟接收天线的相位误差。我们在仿真和实验场景中证明了我们的 3D 成像算法的有效性。

## 背景感知多模态框架：复杂环境下的雷达小目标检测

原英文标题：A Background-Aware Multimodal Framework for Radar Small-Target Detection in Complex Environments

作者：Haoxuan Xu, Meiguo Gao

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems

出版时间：2026-09-04

原英文摘要：Radar small-target detection in complex low-altitude environments remains challenging because weak target echoes are easily masked by heterogeneous clutter, multipath returns, and environment-dependent interference. To improve target–clutter separability, this paper proposes a background-aware multimodal framework that jointly exploits range–Doppler signal structure information (SSI) and satellite-derived environmental priors. Instead of making decisions from isolated range–Doppler cells or directly fusing radar and map images, the proposed method reformulates detection as a target-centric structural discrimination problem. Candidate locations are first generated by a low-threshold CFAR detector, and local SSI patches are extracted to preserve target-induced range–Doppler morphology. A physics-inspired SSI enhancement module is designed to encode log-compressed magnitude, local background contrast, and range- and Doppler-domain structural gradients. Meanwhile, spatially aligned satellite images and land-cover masks are used to construct environmental priors for characterizing local clutter conditions. An enhanced gated cross-modal fusion module then selectively injects background information into radar SSI features while preserving radar features as the dominant representation. Finally, a multi-scale Transformer-based detection head with cross-scale interaction is employed to jointly model fine-grained SSI morphology and broader contextual consistency. Experiments on measured radar data demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms conventional CFAR, representative CNN-, Transformer-, and graph-based detectors, as well as a previous radar–satellite fusion method, especially in low-false-alarm operating regions. Ablation studies, information-scale experiments, and SCIR-level analysis further verify the effectiveness of the proposed components and the importance of appropriate SSI and environmental context scales.

中文摘要：复杂低空环境中的雷达小目标检测仍然具有挑战性，因为微弱目标回波容易被非均匀杂波、多径回波和依赖环境的干扰所掩盖。为提高目标–杂波可分离性，本文提出一种背景感知多模态框架，该框架联合利用距离–多普勒信号结构信息（SSI）和卫星衍生的环境先验。所提方法不是从孤立的距离–多普勒单元进行决策，也不是直接融合雷达和地图图像，而是将检测重新表述为以目标为中心的结构判别问题。候选位置首先由低阈值CFAR检测器生成，并提取局部SSI块以保留目标引起的距离–多普勒形态。设计了一个受物理启发的SSI增强模块，用于编码对数压缩幅度、局部背景对比度以及距离域和多普勒域的结构梯度。同时，空间对齐的卫星图像和土地覆盖掩膜被用于构建环境先验，以表征局部杂波条件。然后，一个增强的门控跨模态融合模块选择性地将背景信息注入雷达SSI特征，同时保留雷达特征作为主导表示。最后，采用具有跨尺度交互的多尺度基于Transformer的检测头，以联合建模细粒度SSI形态和更广泛的上下文一致性。在实测雷达数据上的实验表明，所提方法优于传统CFAR、具有代表性的基于CNN、Transformer和图网络的检测器，以及先前的雷达–卫星融合方法，尤其是在低虚警工作区域。消融研究、信息尺度实验和SCIR级分析进一步验证了所提组件的有效性以及适当的SSI和环境上下文尺度的重要性。

## 基于时空协同相位解缠与频率轨迹增强的水面微振动检测方法

原英文标题：A Water-Surface Micro-Vibration Detection Method Based on Spatiotemporal Collaborative Phase Unwrapping and Frequency-Trajectory Enhancement

作者：Lu Wang, Bo Wang, Hao Li, Zhigang Shang, Tomoaki Ohtsuki, P. Takis Mathiopoulos

出版来源：IEEE Signal Processing Letters

出版时间：2026-09-02

原英文摘要：When underwater acoustic waves propagate to the water-air interface, they induce minute surface displacements and periodic vibrations. By remotely sensing the associated phase variations, millimeter-wave radar enables cross-medium acquisition of underwater acoustic source information. However, the radar echoes are susceptible to phase wrapping, background noise, and strong interference, making the frequency characteristics of the source signal difficult to extract reliably. To address these issues, this letter proposes a water-surface micro-vibration detection method based on the phase-frequency characteristics of radar echoes. First, temporal continuity and spatial-gradient information of the echo phase are jointly exploited to construct a time-gradient two-dimensional Kalman recursion, thereby achieving phase unwrapping and distortion suppression. Second, rotational-gradient processing is employed to enhance the time-frequency representation, while instantaneous-frequency fragment linking is introduced to restore interrupted trajectories and improve source-frequency estimation accuracy. Finally, a two-stage constant false alarm rate detection method is developed to sequentially suppress strong interference and background noise, enabling reliable detection and decoding of underwater acoustic source information. Parameter studies, ablation experiments, and comparative evaluations on measured data validate the effectiveness of the proposed method. The results demonstrate that it improves frequency-estimation accuracy while effectively reducing the bit error rate and false-alarm probability.

中文摘要：当水下声波传播到水-气界面时，会诱导微小的表面位移和周期性振动。通过遥感相关相位变化，毫米波雷达能够跨介质获取水下声源信息。然而，雷达回波易受相位缠绕、背景噪声和强干扰影响，使得源信号的频率特征难以可靠提取。为解决这些问题，本文提出一种基于雷达回波相位-频率特征的水面微振动检测方法。首先，联合利用回波相位的时间连续性和空间梯度信息，构建时间-梯度二维卡尔曼递推，从而实现相位解缠和畸变抑制。其次，采用旋转梯度处理增强时频表示，同时引入瞬时频率片段链接以恢复中断轨迹并提高源频率估计精度。最后，提出一种两级恒虚警率检测方法，依次抑制强干扰和背景噪声，实现水下声源信息的可靠检测与解码。参数研究、消融实验和实测数据对比评估验证了所提方法的有效性。结果表明，该方法在提高频率估计精度的同时，有效降低了误码率和虚警概率。

## 自适应网格细化 MUSIC：面向车载雷达的低复杂度高分辨率 DOA 估计

原英文标题：Adaptive Grid-Refinement MUSIC for Low-Complexity High-Resolution DOA Estimation in Automotive Radar

作者：Bong-seok Kim, Je-seok Kim, Joong-tae Kim, Si-hyeong Kim, Rock-hyun Choi, Sangdong Kim

出版来源：Journal of Electrical Engineering & Technology

出版时间：2026-09-03

原英文摘要：This paper proposes a low-complexity high-resolution direction of arrival (DOA) estimation technique based on adaptive grid definition algorithm. This method solves the problem of excessive computational amount of full multiple signal classification (MUSIC), which searches the entire angular range in a fine grid. The proposed technique firstly sets the region of interest (ROI) by evaluating the MUSIC spectrum with a coarse search for the entire angular range, and secondly performs a fine search only for the corresponding ROI to find a precise peak. In addition, unnecessary eigenvalue decomposition (EVD) calculation was prevented by calculating the noise subspace matrix calculated in the subspace decomposition process only once and reusing it for coarse and fine search. This reduces the computational complexity of the conventional full MUSIC while maintaining the same resolution as full MUSIC. As a result of the simulation, the proposed technique greatly reduced the amount of computation and delay while maintaining the root mean square error (RMSE) and resolution performance and proved its applicability in real-time and low-power environments.

中文摘要：本文提出了一种基于自适应网格定义算法的低复杂度高分辨率波达方向（DOA）估计技术。该方法解决了全多重信号分类（MUSIC）计算量过大的问题，全MUSIC在整个角度范围内以细网格进行搜索。所提技术首先通过在整个角度范围内进行粗搜索来评估MUSIC谱，从而设定感兴趣区域（ROI），其次仅对相应ROI执行细搜索以找到精确峰值。此外，通过仅计算一次在子空间分解过程中计算得到的噪声子空间矩阵，并将其复用于粗搜索和细搜索，避免了不必要的特征值分解（EVD）计算。这在保持与全MUSIC相同分辨率的同时，降低了传统全MUSIC的计算复杂度。仿真结果表明，所提技术在保持均方根误差（RMSE）和分辨率性能的同时，大幅减少了计算量和延迟，并证明了其在实时和低功耗环境中的适用性。

原文链接：https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42835-026-02914-6

However, the fusion accuracy and reliability will be inevitably degraded when the sensing performance of radar or camera degrades in practical applications. To address the degraded perception caused by asymmetric degradation of multimodal sensor data, we propose an uncertainty-aware 4D mmWave radar–camera fusion method for accurate, reliable and robust environmental perception. Comprehensive experiments have been implemented on a large multi-task water-surface perception dataset to compare our method with several advanced learning methods. ## 基于 STFT 谱图滑动相消的汽车雷达电磁干扰抑制

原英文标题：Electromagnetic Interference Mitigation in Automotive Radar by STFT Spectrogram Sliding Cancellation

作者：Youlong Weng, Guangzhi Chen, Jingxuan Chen, Zhiyu Jia, Zongfei Zhou, Donglin Su

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Electromagnetic Compatibility

出版时间：2026-09-02

原英文摘要：The massive deployment of frequency modulated continuous wave (FMCW) radars in autonomous vehicles will lead to severe electromagnetic interference issues. Mutual interference mitigation is vital for ensuring the detection performance of automotive FMCW radars. Existing methods struggle to maintain a balance between interference mitigation performance and computational efficiency in complex scenarios with multiple interferences, which restricts the applicability in practical scenarios requiring rapid response. To address the predicament, this article proposes an efficient method for mutual interference mitigation among automotive FMCW radars using the short-time Fourier transform (STFT) spectrogram sliding cancellation. The interferences and target signals exhibit different time–frequency characteristics. After sliding the STFT spectrogram along the time dimension, the intensity of interference bins fluctuates dramatically while that of target bins remains virtually unchanged. Therefore, this intensity variation is employed to detect and cancel interference bins in the STFT domain. The effectiveness of the proposed method is validated through both simulations and experimental results in different interference scenarios. The results show that the proposed method can effectively eliminate the interferences and recover the target signals even if 100% of the samples are contaminated by the interference, and it is computationally efficient.

中文摘要：调频连续波（FMCW）雷达在自动驾驶汽车中的大规模部署将导致严重的电磁干扰问题。互干扰抑制对于保障车载 FMCW 雷达的检测性能至关重要。现有方法在存在多个干扰的复杂场景中难以在干扰抑制性能与计算效率之间保持平衡，这限制了其在需要快速响应的实际场景中的适用性。为解决这一困境，本文提出一种使用短时傅里叶变换（STFT）谱图滑动对消的车载 FMCW 雷达间互干扰高效抑制方法。干扰与目标信号表现出不同的时频特性。沿时间维度滑动 STFT 谱图后，干扰时频单元的强度剧烈波动，而目标时频单元的强度几乎保持不变。因此，利用这种强度变化在 STFT 域中检测并消除干扰时频单元。所提方法的有效性通过不同干扰场景下的仿真和实验结果得到验证。结果表明，即使 100% 的样本被干扰污染，所提方法也能有效消除干扰并恢复目标信号，且计算效率高。

## 基于毫米波 MIMO FMCW 雷达的明渠水槽表面流速感知实验研究

原英文标题：Experimental study of surface velocity sensing in an open-channel flume using millimeter-wave MIMO FMCW radar

作者：Takumi Matsuda, Hiroyoshi Yamada, Hiroyasu Yasuda

出版来源：IEICE Transactions on Communications

出版时间：2026-09-01

原英文摘要：Accurate measurement of surface-flow velocity is essential for improving our understanding of river hydraulics and enhancing the accuracy of flood prediction. However, the response characteristics of millimeter-wave FMCW radar for river-surface flow under constrained open-channel conditions have not yet been fully clarified. This study evaluates the performance of millimeter-wave FMCW radar in a controlled open-channel environment under various channel conditions. Measurements were conducted at different grazing angles and under two channel-slope conditions, and the Doppler velocities obtained from the radar were compared with reference measurements acquired using a Laser Doppler Velocimeter (LDV). The radar signals were further processed using Moving Target Indicator (MTI) filtering and non-coherent integration, and the resulting range-Doppler maps were analyzed for each experimental condition. The results showed that the dominant radar response was generally consistent with the LDV-derived surface-flow velocity. The received power at the LDV measurement locations tended to increase with grazing angle, whereas smaller grazing angles provided a wider observable area, indicating a practical trade-off in selecting the observation geometry. Additional velocity components that were not captured by the LDV were also observed under sidewall-affected conditions and in cases with installed blocks. These observations suggest that sidewall- and obstacle-related wave and/or clutter effects can contaminate the range-Doppler response in open-channel measurements. Overall, the results demonstrate the potential of millimeter-wave FMCW radar as a practical contactless sensing technique for surface-flow measurements and clarify several experimental factors that should be considered in deployment.

## 基于跟踪集成 InISAR 与预训练元学习的小样本雷达人员识别

原英文标题：Few-Shot Radar Person Identification via Tracking-Integrated InISAR and Pre-Trained Meta-Learning

作者：Jeffee Hsiung, Rengin Torun, S. Hamed Javadi, Hichem Sahli

出版来源：EUSIPCO 2026

出版时间：2026-09-01

原英文摘要：—Radar-based person identification (PID) offers a privacy-preserving alternative to vision systems, yet practical deployment remains difficult. Conventional angle-of-arrival processing produces sparse, noisy point clouds affected by motion defocusing and multipath artefacts, while existing learning methods require large labeled datasets, making adaptation to new individuals impractical. We address these challenges through a unified framework combining tracking-integrated Interferometric Inverse Synthetic Aperture Radar (InISAR) with pretrained meta-learning. The InISAR pipeline reconstructs highresolution 3D human point clouds while suppressing multipath ghosts, achieving 73.78% IoU and <5.5 cm height error on motion capture validation.
