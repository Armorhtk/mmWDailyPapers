## 4D Radar-Camera Vector Map SLAM：动态目标移除掩膜与两阶段回环检测 [J]

原英文标题：4D Radar-Camera Vector Map SLAM with Dynamic Object Removal Mask and Two-Stage Loop Detection

作者：Minseong Choi, Jeonguk Kang, Seungho Han

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement

出版时间：2026-07-30

原英文摘要：A vector map containing lane information is essential to perform global or local path planning for autonomous driving. Vector maps, also termed as HD maps, are typically developed using high-cost LiDAR or a combination of cameras and deep learning. In this paper, the first complete real-time vector map SLAM system using the emerging 4D radar and low-cost cameras is proposed. First, a Dynamic Object Removal Mask (DORM)-based visual-4D radar odometry is proposed, which incorporates a velocity-adaptive and distance-dependent radius function to ensure robust performance in dynamic urban environments. By considering both the object’s absolute velocity and its distance from the sensor, our method effectively suppresses dynamic features while preserving distant static landmarks. The experimental results indicate that the proposed method outperforms state-of-the-art LiDAR SLAM and other techniques in dynamic scenarios. Second, a two-stage loop detection method is suggested using the vector map generated by the inverse perspective mapping (IPM) and the 4D radar Z projection image. Experimental validation demonstrates that odometry drift is reduced through pose graph optimization-based loop closure. A Supplementary video related to this work is available at the following link: https://youtu.be/pPb2ze24F30.

## 弯曲感知谱图相关用于W波段四旋翼检测：一种物理信息非相干检测框架 [M]

原英文标题：Bending-Aware Spectrogram Correlation for W-Band Quadcopter Detection: A Physics-Informed Non-Coherent Detection Framework

作者：Yael Balal, Natan Steinmetz, Arie Sherenzon, Nezah Balal

出版来源：Sensors

出版时间：2026-08-01

原英文摘要：Small multi-rotor UAVs are difficult radar targets: their radar cross-section is low and aspect-dependent, and their slow body motion overlaps with birds and clutter in Doppler processing. We address W-band (94 GHz) detection with a physics-informed, non-coherent framework that exploits blade flexibility. A compact three-dimensional micro-Doppler model combines counter-rotating rotor kinematics, hub-dependent spatial phase, and a first-order out-of-plane bending term; the rigid rotational signature scales with cos beta and the bending contribution with sin beta in elevation beta. Near zenith, where rigid micro-Doppler collapses toward DC, bending repopulates an observable low-velocity band. Detection uses cosine similarity between magnitude spectrograms, which is robust to the tested oscillator impairments. With a calibrated false-alarm rate (PFA=0.01) and unknown target rotor phase, the detector reaches Pd approximately 0.9 at SNR approximately -14 dB and stays stable for carrier-frequency offsets up to 500 Hz and phase random walk up to 0.2 rad/sample, where an uncompensated matched filter fails; it also retains detection against simulated bird-and-clutter micro-Doppler in the background-dominated regime where an energy detector collapses. A consistency check against measured single-blade no-IQ W-band records reproduces the one-sided time-frequency periodicity under a matched product-detector operator. The result is an interpretable, training-free baseline for phase-limited W-band UAV sensing.

中文摘要：小型多旋翼无人机是困难的雷达目标：其雷达截面低且随视角变化，且其缓慢的机体运动在Doppler处理中与鸟类和杂波重叠。我们提出一种基于物理信息、非相干的检测框架，利用叶片柔性来处理W波段（94 GHz）检测问题。一个紧凑的三维微Doppler模型结合了对转旋翼运动学、轮毂相关空间相位以及一阶离面弯曲项；刚性旋转特征随仰角beta按cos beta缩放，而弯曲贡献按sin beta缩放。在天顶附近，刚性微Doppler向DC塌缩时，弯曲重新填充了一个可观测的低速带。检测采用幅度谱图之间的余弦相似性，该相似性对测试的振荡器损伤具有鲁棒性。在校准的虚警率（PFA=0.01）且未知目标旋翼相位的情况下，检测器在SNR约-14 dB时达到Pd约0.9，并在载波频率偏移高达500 Hz和相位随机游走高达0.2 rad/sample时保持稳定，而此时未补偿的匹配滤波器失效；在能量检测器失效的背景主导区域，该检测器还能对模拟的鸟类和杂波微Doppler保持检测。与实测的单叶片无IQ W波段记录的交叉验证表明，在匹配乘积检测算子下重现了单侧时频周期性。结果为相位受限的W波段无人机感知提供了一个可解释、无需训练的基础。

## FMCW：220 GHz高分辨率近程成像雷达系统 [J]

原英文标题：A 220-GHz High-Resolution Near-Range FMCW Imaging Radar System

作者：Huimin Liu, Zhang-Cheng Hao, Feng Xie, Chen-Yu Ding, Zhuo-Wei Miao, Wei Hong

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques

出版时间：2026-07-31

原英文摘要：This article presents a compact, fully integrated 220-GHz frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) synthetic aperture radar (SAR) system for high-resolution, large field-of-view 3-D near-range imaging. Employing a monostatic single-input–single-output architecture, the system exploits the short wavelength and ultrawide bandwidth (UWB) of terahertz (THz) waves to achieve millimeter-scale resolution in a $12\times 6\times 3$ cm 3 form factor. The hardware integrates silicon-based transmitter and receiver modules with signal control, frequency synthesis, data acquisition, and power management circuits on a unified platform. A phase-locked loop (PLL) and broadband voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO) generate linear FMCW chirps with 9–14GHz bandwidth, enabling a range resolution of 3cm. To accelerate data acquisition while ensuring wide spatial coverage, an Archimedean spiral scanning trajectory is adopted based on a bistatic spherical wave propagation model. Nonuniform spatial sampling effects are compensated using the Voronoi tessellation, and Stolt interpolation is applied in k-space to enable efficient 3-D image reconstruction. The prototype achieves a lateral resolution of 2.7mm. Experimental results on concealed objects and complex backgrounds demonstrate the system’s superior performance in terms of compactness, cost-effectiveness, imaging speed, and resolution. This radar is suitable for noninvasive inspection, nondestructive testing, and rapid near-field 3-D imaging applications.

中文摘要：本文介绍了一种紧凑型全集成的220 GHz调频连续波(FMCW)合成孔径雷达(SAR)系统，用于高分辨率、大视场三维近距成像。系统采用单站单输入单输出架构，利用太赫兹(THz)波的短波长和超宽带(UWB)特性，在12×6×3 cm³的外形尺寸内实现毫米级分辨率。硬件将硅基发射和接收模块与信号控制、频率合成、数据采集和电源管理电路集成在统一平台上。锁相环(PLL)和宽带压控振荡器(VCO)产生带宽为9–14 GHz的线性FMCW线性调频脉冲，实现3 cm的距离分辨率。为了在保证宽空间覆盖的同时加快数据采集速度，基于双站球面波传播模型采用了阿基米德螺旋扫描轨迹。使用Voronoi剖分补偿非均匀空间采样效应，并在k空间应用Stolt插值以实现高效的三维图像重建。样机实现了2.7 mm的横向分辨率。对隐蔽物体和复杂背景的实验结果证明了该系统在紧凑性、成本效益、成像速度和分辨率方面的优越性能。该雷达适用于非侵入式检查、无损检测以及快速近场三维成像应用。

## 面向天气鲁棒三维目标检测的4D雷达与LiDAR融合框架 [J]

作者：Huaijin Liu, Jixiang Du, Hongbo Zhang, Guoguang Hua, Jiandian Zeng

出版来源：Neural Networks

出版时间：2026-08-01

原英文摘要：As a foundational technology in autonomous driving, 3D object detection plays a critical role by enabling vehicles to perceive their surroundings. However, LiDAR suffers from severe point cloud degradation in adverse weather, while 4D Radar, though more robust, introduces low-resolution and noisy measurements. Fusing LiDAR and 4D Radar is expected to enhance robustness, yet existing methods lack the ability to dynamically adapt to weather-induced sensor degradation and remain vulnerable to radar noise. To address these issues, we propose DDMDGF, a novel LiDAR-4D Radar fusion framework for weather-robust 3D object detection. The framework introduces a Semantic-guided Foreground-aware Denoising (SFD) module, which leverages enhanced semantic feature prediction and hybrid dynamic thresholding to suppress 4D radar noise while preserving critical foreground structures. In addition, we design a Multi-scale Dual-attention Gated Fusion (MDGF) module, which employs parallel intra-modal and inter-modal backbones to extract complementary features, and uses a channel-spatial joint attention gate to adaptively balance modality contributions under varying weather conditions. Extensive experiments on the VoD and K-Radar datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method: DDMDGF achieves +7.3% AP3D and +4.9% APBEV improvements over L4DR on K-Radar, and under the most severe fog level in VoD-Fog, it outperforms L4DR by +1.6%, +1.8%, and +1.5% mAP on cars, pedestrians, and cyclists, respectively.

## 基于深度学习的毫米波雷达非接触式心音图测量方法 [J]

原英文摘要：Reliable phonocardiogram (PCG) signal measurement plays a crucial role in the detection and prevention of cardiovascular diseases. Current mainstream heart sound acquisition methods rely predominantly on stethoscopes or chest-attached sensors. However, these contact-based approaches may cause patient discomfort and are difficult to maintain for long-term stable measurements due to issues such as limited battery life and electrode detachment. Moreover, conventional stethoscopes generally lack the capability for heart sound recording and quantitative analysis, while digital stethoscopes with recording functionality remain prohibitively expensive, limiting their widespread adoption. In this study, we propose a non-contact PCG measurement method based on mmWave radar. The proposed method exploits the high sensitivity of millimeter-wave radar in detecting subtle chest wall vibrations and employs a series of signal separation algorithms to accurately extract cardiac mechanical activity signals from the radio frequency (RF) signals. Subsequently, a deep neural network based on a TCN-enhanced U-Net architecture is designed to achieve end-to-end high-fidelity reconstruction and denoising from mechanical vibration signals to heart sound signals. Experimental results demonstrate that the reconstructed signals achieve a mean absolute error (MAE) of 0.1486, a root mean square error (RMSE) of 0.2740, and a coefficient of determination (R2) of 0.9057 on the test set, successfully recovering the S1 and S2 heart sound components. These results validate the feasibility of the proposed method for non-contact, continuous, and accurate heart sound reconstruction, offering a novel technical pathway for early screening of cardiovascular diseases and non-invasive health assessment.

## 物理信息 PointNet++：基于雷达点云的手势识别 [M]

原英文标题：A physics-informed PointNet + + for hand gesture recognition using radar point cloud

作者：Genyuan Du, Hua Tao, Meiqi Zhou, Xiangqun Zhang, Guolong Cui

出版来源：Scientific Reports

出版时间：2026-08-05

原英文摘要：Hand gesture recognition based on mmWave radar point clouds has excellent potential in the human-computer interaction field. However, conventional approaches often underutilise raw data and fail to incorporate the underlying physical properties of radar echoes, resulting in a poor trade-off between recognition accuracy and robustness. To overcome this limitation, we propose a Physics-Informed PointNet + + Network (PIPN). First, we integrate point cloud density and echo intensity to generate physics prior weights. Second, inter-frame dynamic correlations are calculated from the PointNet + + backbone’s feature extraction. Finally, we fuse the weights for density, intensity, and inter-frame correlation, embed the physical information of radar point clouds into the network’s feature learning, and guide the network’s attention to the most significant spatio-temporal regions. Evaluated on a comprehensive, self-collected mmWave radar dataset comprising 12 distinct gesture categories, our method enhances feature discriminability. It achieves state-of-the-art recognition accuracy of 99.89%, demonstrating a significant improvement over existing methods. Experimental results confirm the feasibility of our method. It proves a practical method for human-computer interaction.

中文摘要：基于毫米波雷达点云的手势识别在人机交互领域具有巨大潜力。然而，传统方法往往未充分利用原始数据，并且未能结合雷达回波的底层物理特性，导致识别精度与鲁棒性之间的权衡不佳。为克服这一局限，我们提出了一种物理信息 PointNet++ 网络（PIPN）。首先，我们融合点云密度和回波强度以生成物理先验权重。其次，从 PointNet++ 主干网络的特征提取中计算帧间动态相关性。最后，我们融合密度、强度和帧间相关性的权重，将雷达点云的物理信息嵌入网络的特征学习中，并引导网络关注最显著的时空区域。在一个包含 12 种不同手势类别的全面自采毫米波雷达数据集上评估，我们的方法增强了特征的判别性，并达到了 99.89% 的最先进识别准确率，相比现有方法有显著提升。实验结果证实了该方法的可行性，为人机交互提供了一种实用方法。

## 使用Mamba的异构毫米波雷达感知自回归预训练 [M]

原英文标题：Autoregressive pre-training for heterogeneous mmWave radar perception using Mamba

作者：Hongliang Chen, Xiaotian Jiang, Kaitai Guo, Yang Zheng, Siqi Pang, Shenghan Ren, Jimin Liang

出版来源：Expert Systems with Applications

出版时间：2026-07-30

原英文摘要：Public mmWave radar datasets are collected under heterogeneous acquisition configurations and processing protocols, leading to systematic geometric and signal-statistical shifts across datasets. Even when the underlying hardware is similar, differences in grid resolution, field-of-view, measurable range/velocity, and the availability of a calibrated Doppler/velocity axis make naive multi-dataset aggregation unreliable and can bias models toward dataset-specific artifacts. We propose MetaARM, a metadata-conditioned self-supervised pre-training framework for heterogeneous mmWave radar perception. MetaARM partitions a 3D radar tensor into non-overlapping 3D patches and trains with causal next-patch prediction under a structured serialization order: tokens are first ordered along the sequence axis and then raster-scanned within each Range-Azimuth plane. To explicitly handle configuration shifts, MetaARM injects acquisition metadata through two coupled mechanisms: Absolute Coordinate Encoding (ACE), which converts discrete patch indices into normalized physical coordinates using resolution and FoV metadata to reduce geometric inconsistency; and FiLM-based conditioning inside Mamba blocks, which calibrates token features before the selective scan according to observable acquisition/configuration metadata.
