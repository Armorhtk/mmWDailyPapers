## 3DCKM：面向低空走廊的大规模多频段三维信道知识图数据集 [M]

原英文标题：3DCKM: A Large-Scale Multiband 3D Channel Knowledge Map Dataset for Low-Altitude Corridors

作者：Chenyang Zhou, Xinyue Jiang, Tong Li, Zhu Xiao, Jie Zhang

出版来源：2026 IEEE International Conference on Communications Workshops (ICC Workshops)

出版时间：2026-05-28

原英文摘要：The emergence of Low-Altitude Corridors (LACs) for unmanned aerial vehicle operations introduces unique challenges for wireless communication, where three-dimensional (3D) air–ground links exhibit strong altitude-dependent channel variations. Accurate modeling of these effects requires 3D channel information that captures the spatial relationship between wireless propagation and the environment. Existing channel datasets remain limited to terrestrial scenarios and lack comprehensive 3D spatial and multiband channel information. To bridge this gap, we propose 3DCKM, a large-scale multiband 3D Channel Knowledge Map dataset designed for LACs. 3DCKM provides extensive coverage across multiple frequency bands from sub-6 GHz to millimeter-wave, capturing detailed channel characteristics at varying altitudes to model altitude-dependent propagation effects. 3DCKM dataset integrates spatially aligned environmental data, including building map, terrain features, and material properties, with rich channel knowledge encompassing path loss, propagation delay, angle of arrival/departure (AoA/AoD), zenith angles (ZoA/ZoD), and multipath interactions. By offering large-scale multiband 3D channel knowledge, 3DCKM enables critical applications such as CKM construction, AI-based channel prediction, and LAC network optimization. The dataset is available at https://github.com/zcy2002zcy/3DCKM-datasets.git.

中文摘要：无人机运行的低空走廊（LAC）的出现为无线通信带来了独特挑战，其中三维（3D）空地链路表现出强烈的依赖于高度的信道变化。准确建模这些效应需要捕捉无线传播与环境之间空间关系的三维信道信息。现有信道数据集仍局限于地面场景，并且缺乏全面的三维空间和多频段信道信息。为填补这一空白，我们提出了3DCKM，一个面向低空走廊的大规模多频段三维信道知识图数据集。3DCKM在从sub-6 GHz到毫米波的多个频段上提供广泛覆盖，在不同高度捕获详细的信道特征，以建模依赖于高度的传播效应。3DCKM数据集集成了空间对齐的环境数据，包括建筑地图、地形特征和材料属性，以及丰富的信道知识，涵盖路径损耗、传播延迟、到达角/离开角（AoA/AoD）、天顶角（ZoA/ZoD）和多径交互。通过提供大规模多频段三维信道知识，3DCKM支持CKM构建、基于AI的信道预测和LAC网络优化等关键应用。该数据集可在 https://github.com/zcy2002zcy/3DCKM-datasets.git 获取。

## 数据高效的图注意力网络（GAT）：利用微多普勒时频谱图的结构化表示进行人体运动感知 [M]

原英文标题：A Data-Efficient Graph Attention Network for Human Motion Sensing Using Micro-Doppler Spectrogram with Structural Representation

作者：Insoo Choi, Youngwook Kim

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Letters

出版时间：2026-07-20

原英文摘要：In this paper, we propose a Graph Attention Network (GAT) for human motion classification by analyzing a structure of spectrograms obtained from radar sensor. Radar sensors provide robust micro-Doppler signatures for activity recognition, which are typically analyzed using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). However, these image-based models struggle with constraints such as the data quantity and short observation-time, which are common in real-world sensor applications. This study proposes a paradigm by representing spectrograms measured by radar sensor not as images, but as graph structures where energy patterns form nodes and their spatiotemporal relationships form edges. We introduce a GAT to efficiently learn these structural relationships. We validate this approach on a diverse dataset comprising both human subjects and robots using different radar sensors, demonstrating the model's high generalization. Also, we show that optimizing graph connectivity is essential for maximizing performance. Our method achieves approximately 6-10% accuracy improvement over other deep learning methods when the observation time is short, proving that this structural-representation approach offers a data-efficient, and alternative for sensor-based classification.

中文摘要：本文提出一种图注意力网络（GAT），通过分析雷达传感器获取的时频谱图结构进行人体运动分类。雷达传感器为活动识别提供鲁棒的微多普勒特征，通常用卷积神经网络（CNNs）进行分析。然而，这些基于图像的模型受限于数据量和观测时间短等实际传感器应用中的常见约束。本研究提出一种新范式：将雷达传感器测量的时频谱图不表示为图像，而表示为图结构，其中能量模式形成节点，其时空关系形成边。我们引入 GAT 来高效学习这些结构关系。我们在包含人类受试者和机器人的多样化数据集上验证了该方法，使用了不同的雷达传感器，展示了模型的高泛化能力。同时，我们表明优化图连接性对于最大化性能至关重要。在观测时间短的情况下，我们的方法相比其他深度学习方法取得了约6-10%的准确率提升，证明这种结构化表示方法为基于传感器的分类提供了一种数据高效且可行的替代方案。

## 结合时域复数差分与双向趋势重建的低复杂度FMCW雷达生命体征估计方法 [M]

原英文标题：A Low-Complexity FMCW Radar Vital-Sign Estimation Method Combining Time-Domain Complex Differencing and Bidirectional Trend Reconstruction

作者：Yuhang Yin, Lin Guo, Zuxin Luo, Qinghua Cui, Xiangkui Wan

出版来源：Eng

出版时间：2026-07-18

原英文摘要：This paper proposes a lightweight vital-sign detection framework based on Frequency-Modulated Continuous Wave (FMCW) radar. By integrating time-domain complex differencing and adaptive trend reconstruction, the proposed framework mitigates distortions in chest micro-motion signals caused by multipath reflections, radar cross-section (RCS) variations, high-frequency impulsive noise, and body-motion artifacts in practical monitoring scenarios. The framework first employs a Time-Domain Complex Differencing and Sliding Accumulated Energy (TDCD-SAE) algorithm to precisely lock onto the target range-bin, followed by phase-difference extraction utilizing Complex Conjugate Multiplication (CCM). To eliminate non-physiological interference, an Adaptive Threshold-Based Bidirectional Trend Reconstruction (AT-BTR) algorithm is introduced to restore the corrupted phase profile. The optimized phase signal is converted into chest-wall displacement, followed by body motion detection to realize the joint estimation of the respiration rate and the heart rate. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed system achieves a high precision, yielding low absolute errors of 1.24/1.71 BPM (supine) and 1.39/2.24 BPM (lateral), thereby validating its efficacy and robustness across diverse sleeping postures in resource-constrained environments.

中文摘要：本文提出了一种基于调频连续波（FMCW）雷达的轻量级生命体征检测框架。通过集成时域复数差分与自适应趋势重建，该框架减轻了实际监测场景中由多径反射、雷达横截面（RCS）变化、高频脉冲噪声以及身体运动伪影所导致的胸部微动信号失真。该框架首先采用时域复数差分与滑动累加能量（TDCD-SAE）算法精确锁定目标距离单元，随后利用复共轭乘法（CCM）提取相位差。为消除非生理干扰，引入基于自适应阈值的双向趋势重建（AT-BTR）算法以恢复受损的相位轮廓。优化后的相位信号被转换为胸壁位移，随后进行身体运动检测，以实现呼吸率和心率的联合估计。实验结果表明，所提系统实现了高精度，在仰卧和侧卧姿势下分别获得1.24/1.71 BPM和1.39/2.24 BPM的低绝对误差，从而验证了其在资源受限环境中跨多种睡眠姿势的有效性和鲁棒性。

## 一种基于微扫描调制的前视成像雷达图像序列超分辨率重建方法 [M]

原英文标题：A Sequential Image Super-Resolution Reconstruction Method for Forward-Looking Imaging Radar With Micro-Scanning Modulation

作者：Hongfei Lian, Yanwen Jiang, Jiuming Zhou, V. Y. Tsviatkou, A. V. Gusinsky, Hongqi Fan

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems

出版时间：2026-07-15

原英文摘要：Super-resolution reconstruction technology of image sequences with spatial modulation provides the capability to surpass the Rayleigh resolution limit of forward-looking imaging radar (FLIR). However, existing imaging models—primarily borrowed from optical imaging systems—are incompatible with radar image sequences due to fundamental differences: optical scenes are inherently continuous with real-valued representations, while radar scenes exhibit sparse scattering properties with complex-valued data. Moreover, state-of-the-art reconstruction methods, which typically rely on batch processing and grid-based model, suffer from high computational complexity and significant performance degradation under low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) conditions, owing to their insufficient utilization of dynamic spatial modulation information. This paper first establishes a gridless imaging model for FLIR with micro-scanning spatial modulation by exploiting the inherent sparsity of radar scenes. Based on this model, we formulate a general formal super-resolution reconstruction problem for image sequences as a corresponding inverse problem. Subsequently, we propose a sequential “projecting-after-filtering” (SPAF) super-resolution reconstruction method within the Bayesian filtering framework. This approach effectively integrates spatial modulation and dynamic variation information across the image sequences, thereby providing the potential to reduce computational complexity and enhance reconstruction performance. Meanwhile, the probability hypothesis density (PHD) image, naturally derived from the filtering step within the proposed framework by employing the cardinalized probability hypothesis density (CPHD) filter, characterizes the spatial existence probability of scattering points, thereby enabling a more comprehensive scene representation. Furthermore, we develop two particle filter implementations: the standard sequential version (SPAF-PF) and a parallelized variant (SPAF-PPF) that leverages multithreading to accelerate computation. Simulations and experiments demonstrate that our method exhibits superior computational efficiency while achieving better reconstruction performance than prior arts.

中文摘要：具有空间调制的图像序列的超分辨率重建技术提供了超越前视成像雷达（FLIR）瑞利分辨率极限的能力。然而，现有的成像模型主要借用自光学成像系统，由于基本差异而与雷达图像序列不兼容：光学场景本质上是连续的，具有实值表示，而雷达场景表现出具有复值数据的稀疏散射特性。此外，现有最先进的重建方法通常依赖于批处理和基于网格的模型，由于未能充分利用动态空间调制信息，在低信噪比（SNR）条件下计算复杂度高且性能显著下降。本文首先利用雷达场景的固有稀疏性，为具有微扫描空间调制的FLIR建立了一个无网格成像模型。基于该模型，我们将图像序列的一般形式超分辨率重建问题表述为相应的逆问题。随后，我们在贝叶斯滤波框架内提出了一种顺序“先滤波后投影”（SPAF）超分辨率重建方法。该方法有效整合了图像序列中的空间调制和动态变化信息，从而具有降低计算复杂度和提升重建性能的潜力。同时，通过采用势概率假设密度（CPHD）滤波器，从所提框架中的滤波步骤自然导出的概率假设密度（PHD）图像表征了散射点的空间存在概率，从而实现更全面的场景表示。此外，我们开发了两种粒子滤波器实现：标准顺序版本（SPAF-PF）和利用多线程加速计算的并行化变体（SPAF-PPF）。仿真和实验表明，我们的方法在实现优于先前技术的重建性能的同时，具有更高的计算效率。

原文链接：https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11611562

原英文摘要：The angular-domain channel knowledge map (A-CKM) stores channel path angles distributed in a target area, which is particularly useful for multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) transmission. However, the spatial non-stationary nature of angular features poses a challenge to A-CKM construction, especially in complex propagation environments. For this problem, we propose a framework for A-CKM construction, which consists of key steps including subregion partition, spatial interpolation, and sampling optimization. Specifically, the target area is firstly partitioned into multiple disjoint subregions based on cluster-level angular features, ensuring local environmental similarity in every subregion. Subsequently, a subregion-based inverse distance weighting (IDW) interpolation method is proposed to construct the A-CKM from sparsely sampled measurements. In addition, a sequential sampling optimization scheme is proposed to improve the sampling efficiency, while enabling continuous map refinement over time. Simulations validate the effectiveness of our proposed A-CKM construction scheme over conventional approaches. Moreover, through a case study of MIMO beamforming, we further demonstrate that the proposed CKM construction method effectively enhances communication performance.

原英文摘要：DeeperRadar is a radar-centric, sensor-stack-conditioned framework that co-designs radar sensing and multi-modal 3D detection for autonomous mobility by learning a sparse acquisition pattern end-to-end with the fusion model. A learnable MIMO design module is trained end-to-end within a fusion network that operates directly on raw radar ADC data together with camera images and LiDAR point clouds. During training, the design module is supervised by the other sensors, enabling the system to learn both which receiver antennas to activate and the effective number of them. At deployment, the design module is removed and replaced by the learned sparse subsampling mask, leaving the downstream model architecture unchanged. Evaluated on the RADIal dataset, DeeperRadar discovers sparse, task-aware radar configurations that match or exceed full-array baselines while using fewer receivers, potentially reducing radar cost and integration complexity. These results show that learned optimal MIMO radar design depends on the fusion stack and the downstream perception task.

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Letters

出版时间：2026-07-16

原英文摘要：This study proposes to enhance the range accuracy of radar sensor through a deep learning approach. As object-detection needs in automotive, industrial, and medical applications evolve, radar sensors are becoming increasingly important for their cost-effectiveness and robust performance across varied environmental conditions. Despite these advantages, radar's measurement accuracy often falls, especially when measuring closely spaced objects. Conventional FMCW radar range estimation relies on detecting peaks in the range profile obtained via Fourier Transform (FT). However, FT suffers from the limited bandwidth as well as the existence of sidelobes in the transformed domain. Therefore, the range profile is merged or distorted when target peaks have insufficient separation. By applying a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to the radar range profile, we aim to improve range estimation accuracy when two targets are closely spaced so that peaks are merged or distorted. The proposed method shows a reduction in mean absolute error compared to traditional techniques and other models, enhancing the radar's capability to precisely measure the range of targets. The results demonstrate that the CNN-based approach can improve sensor range accuracy by an average of 21.2%, suggesting potential applications for analyzing muti-scatterer targets.

中文摘要：本研究提出通过深度学习方法提升雷达传感器的距离精度。随着汽车、工业和医疗应用中对目标检测需求的演进，雷达传感器因其成本效益以及在多变环境条件下的稳健性能而愈发重要。尽管有这些优势，雷达的测量精度往往不足，尤其在测量近距离物体时。传统的 FMCW 雷达距离估计依赖于检测通过傅里叶变换（FT）得到的距离剖面中的峰值。然而，FT 受限于有限带宽以及变换域中旁瓣的存在。因此，当目标峰值间距不足时，距离剖面会合并或畸变。通过将卷积神经网络（CNN）应用于雷达距离剖面，我们旨在提升两个目标相距很近导致峰值合并或畸变时的距离估计精度。与传统技术及其他模型相比，所提方法在平均绝对误差上有所降低，增强了雷达精确测量目标距离的能力。结果表明，基于 CNN 的方法能够平均提升传感器距离精度 21.2%，暗示了其在分析多散射体目标方面的潜在应用。

## 基于毫米波雷达的步态分析：使用点云Transformer的骨架方法 [M]

原英文标题：Gait Analysis Using mmWave Radar: A Skeleton-Based Approach with Point Cloud Transformer

作者：Jiahao Tang, Shuting Hu, Siyang Cao, Jennifer Barton, Melvin G. Hector, Mindy J. Fain, Nima Toosizadeh

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Medical Robotics and Bionics

出版时间：2026-07-16

原英文摘要：This study presents a skeleton-based approach for gait analysis using millimeter-Wave radar sensors. mmWave radar is non-intrusive, privacy-preserving, unaffected by lighting conditions, and both cost-effective and energy-efficient. Current radar-based gait analysis methods typically use micro-Doppler signatures to identify walking phases and extract features. In contrast, our approach leverages a pose-estimation model to reconstruct the human skeleton from radar point cloud data, enabling comprehensive full-body analysis. This enables a more intuitive and detailed gait analysis while also allowing for direct signal-level comparisons with wearable sensor-based approaches. In our study, we recruited 78 participants and conducted gait tests across four distinct environments to evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed method. The results showed that gait features, including stride time, stride length, and stride velocity, exhibited good to excellent Intraclass Correlation Coefficients (ICCs) when compared with wearable sensors. Additionally, we are the first to analyze sub-phase features such as swing, stance, and double support using a radar system. ## Wave2Body：将毫米波人体姿态估计重新构想为雷达到人体令牌翻译 [Preprint]

作者：Bo Liang, Chen Gong, Wei Gao, Chenren Xu

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-07-21

原英文摘要：Millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar enables privacy-friendly human sensing, but its sparse point clouds are physical measurements of view-dependent electromagnetic reflections and only indirectly characterize body articulation. Recovering a complete 3D pose from such partial, geometry-dependent observations is therefore under-constrained. Existing methods directly regress joint coordinates from paired radar-pose data, relying on the same limited paired supervision to learn radar perception, human-body structure, and their alignment. This coupling can encourage dataset-specific shortcuts under ambiguous radar observations. We propose Wave2Body, a radar-to-body token translation framework that decouples these learning targets using a self-supervised mmWave tokenizer, a pretrained compositional body tokenizer that defines the output space, and a lightweight translator between them. Experiments on M4Human and mmBody show that Wave2Body achieves stronger cross-domain generalization than previous methods while incurring much lower computational costs for training and inference. All the code and experiment results are publicly available at https://github.com/Galaxywalk/Wave2Body.
