## 基于雷达信号的睡眠呼吸暂停检测的新型对比深度学习框架

原英文标题：A New Contrastive Deep Learning Framework for Sleep Apnea Detection from Radar Signal

作者：Renjie Cheng, Shaolin Liao, Ming Zhang, Jiong Liang, Yaoqin Xie, Yi Huang, Chaijie Duan, Hanrong Cheng

出版来源：IEEE Internet of Things Journal

出版时间：2026-08-25

原英文摘要：Obstructive Sleep Apnea and Hypopnea (OSAH) is the most prevalent sleep-related breathing disorder, which can significantly affect human health and cause economic burdens. Radar-based sensing provides a non-contact and continuous monitoring solution, making it a promising alternative to the current gold standard, polysomnography (PSG), for overnight sleep apnea detection. However, the study of apnea detection with radar data is still insufficient. Moreover, the intensive and time-consuming manual annotation of the overnight data has limited the scale of the labeled data sets, which makes the apnea detection task more challenging. In this paper, we propose a self-supervised learning framework that incorporates a Distributional Learnable Augmentation (DLA) module for adaptive data augmentation and a Bidirectional Phase Mamba (BPMamba) network to capture long-range temporal dependencies in respiratory signals. The proposed model was evaluated on an experimentally collected dataset, achieving a classification accuracy of 92.69% and an F1-score of 92.08%. Notably, when trained with only 10% of labeled data, the proposed method significantly outperformed fully supervised baseline models in terms of accuracy, demonstrating strong robustness under limited labeled data conditions and effectively reducing the reliance on manual annotation.

## LFMCW 雷达时间 FIR 滤波弱目标检测方法

原英文标题：A Weak Target Detection Method for LFMCW Radar with Temporal FIR Filter

作者：Mei Zhang, Yongbo Zhao, Derui Tang

出版来源：Journal of Information and Intelligence

出版时间：2026-08-26

原英文摘要：Due to the unique anti-capture capability and absence of range blind zones, linear frequency modulated continuous wave (LFMCW) radar has been widely used in various fields. The range estimation for LFMCW radar typically employs the discrete Fourier transform (DFT) technique. However, when a weak target is adjacent to the strong target, the DFT exhibits poor Doppler filtering performance. In this paper, we propose a weak target detection method for LFMCW radar with temporal finite impulse response (FIR) filter. To solve the missed detection caused by the strong clutter, we design a time-domain FIR filter tailored to the clutter environment, which performs the time-domain filtering on the beat signal to extract the target’s range. Similarly, when the weak target is masked by an adjacent strong target, we extract the characteristics of the strong target to reconstruct it. Then, the FIR filter is developed to suppress the strong target to accurately locate the weak target. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed algorithms improve the detection probability of weak targets while ensuring the accuracy and robustness of target estimation.

中文摘要：由于线性调频连续波（LFMCW）雷达具有独特的抗截获能力且不存在距离盲区，已被广泛应用于各个领域。LFMCW 雷达的距离估计通常采用离散傅里叶变换（DFT）技术。然而，当弱目标与强目标相邻时，DFT 的 Doppler 滤波性能较差。本文提出一种基于时间有限脉冲响应（FIR）滤波的 LFMCW 雷达弱目标检测方法。为解决强杂波引起的漏检，我们针对杂波环境设计了时域 FIR 滤波器，对差频信号进行时域滤波以提取目标距离。类似地，当弱目标被相邻强目标遮蔽时，我们提取强目标特征进行重构，进而设计 FIR 滤波器抑制强目标，以准确定位弱目标。仿真结果表明，所提算法在保证目标估计精度与鲁棒性的同时，提高了弱目标的检测概率。

## 基于电磁模型与反演方法的雷达手势识别

原英文摘要：—Radars offer robust gesture recognition capabilities over traditional vision, capacitance, and ultrasound sensors, with flexibility in preserving user privacy, sensing in different lighting conditions, higher temporal resolution, and sensing through materials. However, raw radar signals are cluttered, difficult to interpret, and strongly dependent on the radar hardware and scene configuration, which often forces gesture recognizers to be retrained for each new radar and environment. To address these limitations, we build on a full-wave electromagnetic radar model and inversionbased approach to calibrate and normalize radar measurements into a physically meaningful Green’s function that is independent of the radar hardware and background scene. Our results achieve classification accuracies of 90.58%, 97.78%, and 98.54% for the full set, and subsets of 12 and 8 gestures, respectively, confirming the approach’s robustness and flexibility.

## 基于多技术融合的室内热舒适智能调控方法

原英文摘要：Indoor thermal comfort significantly affects human health, productivity, and energy consumption in buildings, yet conventional HVAC systems often fail to accommodate dynamic occupancy changes and individual variability. This study proposes an intelligent air conditioning control system that integrates millimeter-wave radar sensing, CFD numerical simulation, machine learning-based thermal comfort prediction, and fuzzy logic control into a unified closed-loop framework. Unlike previous studies that treat sensing, simulation, and control separately, this system enables real-time occupant monitoring (count, spatial distribution, and activity type) combined with CFD-generated thermal fields and data-driven comfort prediction to achieve adaptive HVAC regulation. The Random Forest model with five-category thermal sensation vote (TSV) output achieved the highest balanced performance, with air temperature and metabolic rate identified as the dominant predictors. The system was validated through three consecutive simulation scenarios with varying occupant counts and activity types, demonstrating convergence to thermal neutrality within one to two control iterations; the proportion of occupants reaching the neutral thermal sensation range increased from 0% to 100% (Scenario 1), from 16.7% to 66.7% (Scenario 2), and to 100% (Scenario 3). CFD simulations were validated against experimental measurements with R² > 0.8 for most operating conditions, though physical implementation remains future work. This research provides a novel multi-technology integration framework for occupant-centric HVAC control, offering practical pathways for energy conservation and carbon reduction in building operations.

## 低可见度环境下用于物体重建的自主mmWave雷达UAV感知系统

原英文摘要：We develop a mmWave radar-based autonomous UAV sensing system for 3D reconstruction in a low-visibility environment. By preserving the full spatial information of radar intensity data, the model learns a mapping from radar voxel inputs to object structure representations and the surrounding representations, despite the fact that radar data measurements are limited by low spatial resolution, sparsity, and noise in an indoor environment. This enhanced sensing capability is better suited for real-world exploration than the previous works, which either can only conduct near-ground scans restricted by ground robots, or rely on slow synthetic aperture radar (SAR), not suitable for small UAVs with their high vibration interfering with the SAR process. Our experimental results show that the proposed method can recover main objects’ shapes and overall spatial distribution from real measured radar data carried by an UAV, demonstrating the feasibility of using mmWave radar for UAV-based real-world 3D reconstruction in a low-visibility environment.

## 基于MIMO FMCW雷达的空中书写连续数字序列识别

原英文标题：Continuous Digit Sequence Recognition in Air-Writing Using MIMO FMCW Radar

作者：Keonwoo Kim, Chanul Park, Seongwook Lee

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Journal

出版时间：2026-08-26

原英文摘要：Radar sensor-based air-writing can enable contactless human-machine interaction by recognizing digits written in mid-air. However, while extensive research has focused on isolated digit recognition using a single radar sensor, studies on continuous digit sequences remain limited, despite its greater practical utility in real-world deployments. To this end, we propose a deep learning-based method designed to automatically recognize sequential digits using a radar sensor. In the proposed method, a multiple-input multiple output frequency-modulated continuous-wave radar sensor is used to estimate the range, velocity, and angle of the moving hand. Subsequently, density-based spatial clustering of applications with noise (DBSCAN) is applied within each frame group to suppress sparse noise and clutter-induced points. The dominant hand-motion cluster is then selected across consecutive frame groups, and a single representative point is extracted from each selected cluster. These representative points are arranged chronologically to reconstruct a compact air-writing trajectory, which is used as the input to a convolutional neural network (CNN)–bidirectional long short-term memory (BiLSTM) network with connectionist temporal classification (CTC). In the network, the CNN layers extract local temporal features from the sequence of coordinates, and the BiLSTM layer hidden states capture long-range temporal dependencies in the writing motion. The CTC decoder then automatically identifies digit sequences without manual segmentation of individual digits. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method achieves a digit error rate of 3.22% and a sequence error rate of 11.20% in continuous air-writing digit recognition.

中文摘要：基于雷达传感器的空中书写通过识别空中书写的数字，可实现非接触式人机交互。然而，尽管已有大量研究聚焦于使用单一雷达传感器进行孤立数字识别，针对连续数字序列的研究仍然较少，而后者在实际部署中具有更高的实用价值。为此，我们提出了一种基于深度学习的方法，旨在利用雷达传感器自动识别连续数字。在所提方法中，使用多输入多输出调频连续波（MIMO FMCW）雷达传感器估计运动手部的距离、速度和角度。随后，在每个帧组内应用基于密度的带噪声空间聚类（DBSCAN）算法，以抑制稀疏噪声和杂波产生的点。接着在连续帧组中选取主导的手部运动聚类簇，并从每个选定簇中提取单个代表点。这些代表点按时间先后排列以重构紧凑的空中书写轨迹，并作为带有联结主义时间分类（CTC）的卷积神经网络（CNN）-双向长短期记忆（BiLSTM）网络的输入。在该网络中，CNN 层从坐标序列中提取局部时序特征，BiLSTM 层的隐藏状态捕获书写动作中的长程时序依赖关系。随后 CTC 解码器自动识别数字序列，无需对单个数字进行手动分割。实验结果表明，该方法在连续空中书写数字识别中实现了 3.22% 的数字错误率和 11.20% 的序列错误率。

## DART-VBE：面向雷达三维重建的联合速度偏差估计与隐式多普勒层析

原英文标题：DART-VBE: Joint Velocity-Bias Estimation and Implicit Doppler Tomography for Radar 3-D Reconstruction

作者：Yanping Wang, Jianan Peng, Yang Li, Yun Lin, Wenjie Shen, Wen Jiang, Zechao Bai

出版来源：IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters

出版时间：2026-08-28

原英文摘要：Three-dimensional reconstruction with compact millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar is challenging due to sparse measurements and limited angular resolution. DART (Doppler Aided Radar Tomography) addresses this problem by exploiting ego-motion-induced Doppler diversity to learn a continuous neural radar field and synthesize range-Doppler (RD) observations from novel viewpoints. However, when applying DART to selfcollected radar data, we observe geometric artifacts whose origins are not directly revealed by the implicit reconstruction pipeline or the final RD reconstruction loss. To investigate this issue, we develop a simulation-based analysis method tailored to DARTlike implicit Doppler tomography. Using fixed RD observations, we independently perturb the position, velocity, and orientation priors and further analyze their coupled effect under odometry drift. The controlled results show distinct degradation patterns and, under the tested perturbations, reveal a pronounced Doppler misalignment when the velocity prior is erroneous. We further show that this error affects not only the Doppler projection but also the Doppler-constrained 3-D sampling support, causing reconstruction gradients to be assigned to incorrect spatial locations. Based on these findings, we propose DART-VBE, a frame-wise velocity-bias estimation framework that introduces a learnable speed correction into the differentiable DART rendering pipeline before RD sampling. To make the correction identifiable and better decouple motion-induced errors from scene-field parameters, we combine the original RD reconstruction loss with a Doppler centroid alignment loss and a motion regularization term. Experiments on public and self-collected datasets show that DART-VBE generally improves RD synthesis quality, reduces Doppler centroid error and Chamfer Distance, and produces more consistent 3-D radar reconstructions than the original DART baseline. Speed-bias simulation studies further show that the learned correction provides a physically meaningful estimate of the injected velocity bias.

## 相机与多UWB雷达信号动态融合：稳健的非接触心跳估计

原英文标题：Dynamic Fusion of Camera and Multi-UWB Radar Signals Measurements for Robust Non-Contact Heartbeat Estimation

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement

原英文摘要：Non-contact vital sign measurement is crucial for improving health monitoring instruments, demonstrating high accuracy while ensuring user convenience and comfort in clinical, home, and remote environments. While camera-based remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) and ultra-wideband (UWB) radar micro-motion sensing offer promising approaches, each faces limitations from environmental factors—cameras from illumination and skin variability, radars from motion artifacts and multipath effects. In this paper, we propose a dynamic deep fusion approach for integrating synchronized RGB and RF signals, combining cross-modal complementary features to reconstruct PPG waveforms under realistic conditions. We also introduce a multimodal instrumentation benchmark dataset—the first combining visual and multi-type UWB radar measurements (one FMCW and two IR-UWB for sensing diversity) with ground-truth PPG— collected from 17 subjects at varying distances (0.5–1.5 m), angles (−45° to +45°), and activities (resting, phone use).
