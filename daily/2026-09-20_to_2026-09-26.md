## 面向自动驾驶的4D雷达感知算法：综述

原英文标题：4D Radar Perception Algorithms for Autonomous Driving: A Review

作者：Xumin Wu, Jun Zhou, Jilin Mei, Chen Min, Yu Hu

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-16

原英文摘要：Research on 4D millimeter-wave radar perception algorithms has flourished in recent years, extending from signal processing and object detection to semantic segmentation, motion estimation, occupancy prediction, and dynamic scene reconstruction. This review organizes the field according to the evolution of perception tasks and algorithms. It first introduces radar fundamentals, data representations, and quality-enhancement methods, and then reviews object-level perception, motion and localization, local and dense spatial perception, and dynamic scene understanding. Across these directions, we compare radar-only learning, multimodal fusion, and cross-modal supervision and knowledge distillation. Particular attention is paid to how elevation, Doppler measurements, and radar physical priors are exploited across tasks. We further summarize the task coverage, input data, annotations, and evaluation protocols of existing datasets, clarifying the empirical support for different research directions. Finally, we discuss the common challenges and future directions of 4D radar perception for autonomous driving. This review provides a task-oriented perspective on the transition from sparse object perception to dynamic spatial understanding.

中文摘要：近年来，4D毫米波雷达感知算法的研究蓬勃发展，已从信号处理和目标检测扩展到语义分割、运动估计、占据预测和动态场景重建。本综述根据感知任务与算法的演进组织该领域。首先介绍雷达基础、数据表示和质量增强方法，然后回顾目标级感知、运动与定位、局部与稠密空间感知以及动态场景理解。在这些方向上，我们比较仅雷达学习、多模态融合以及跨模态监督与知识蒸馏。特别关注俯仰、多普勒测量和雷达物理先验如何在不同任务中被利用。我们进一步总结现有数据集的任务覆盖、输入数据、标注和评估协议，阐明不同研究方向的实证支持。最后，我们讨论面向自动驾驶的4D雷达感知的共同挑战与未来方向。本综述为从稀疏目标感知到动态空间理解的转变提供了任务导向的视角。

## 基于多模态传感器信息融合的复杂场景可靠目标识别方法

原英文标题：A Reliable Target Recognition Method for Complex Scenarios Based on Multimodal Sensor Information Fusion

作者：Biao Jia, Dong Wei, Lei Si, Zhongbin Wang, Xin Li, Yang Liu

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Journal

出版时间：2026-09-17

原英文摘要：The harsh conditions of underground coal mines necessitate enhancing the reliability and adaptability of mobile robot perception systems within operational scenarios. Addressing core challenges such as LiDAR point cloud failure due to dust and water vapour interference, alongside visual camera imaging distortion under low-illumination conditions,we propose a 3D object detection method based on Radar and Lidar fusion enhanced with target velocity and Radar Cross Section (RCS) information, named VR-LRFusion, which the network takes both Radar and LiDAR data as inputs. To fully leverage the strengths of Radar in terms of velocity and RCS data, velocity encoders and RCS encoders are introduced, and Radar features are enhanced separately through a velocity-RCS attention mechanism. Subsequently, to address the sparsity of the Radar point cloud, the fusion module employs a cross-attention mechanism to integrate LiDAR features with the enhanced Radar features, thereby improving the network’s ability to detect moving obstacles. Experimental validation on the NuScenes public dataset demonstrates that, compared to existing methods, our approach achieves improvements of 2.93% and 2.42% in mAP and NDS metrics, respectively. VR-LRFusion exhibits robust detection performance in dynamic object detection. At the same time, we have constructed a dataset of underground coal mine environments, and our algorithm has demonstrated exceptional adaptability to this environment.

中文摘要：煤矿井下的恶劣条件要求提升移动机器人感知系统在作业场景中的可靠性和适应性。针对粉尘和水汽干扰导致的激光雷达点云失效，以及低照度条件下视觉相机成像畸变等核心挑战，我们提出一种基于雷达和激光雷达融合、并由目标速度和雷达散射截面（RCS）信息增强的3D目标检测方法，命名为VR-LRFusion，该网络同时以雷达和激光雷达数据作为输入。为了充分利用雷达在速度和RCS数据方面的优势，引入了速度编码器和RCS编码器，并通过速度-RCS注意力机制分别增强雷达特征。随后，为解决雷达点云的稀疏性问题，融合模块采用交叉注意力机制将激光雷达特征与增强后的雷达特征进行融合，从而提升网络检测移动障碍物的能力。在NuScenes公开数据集上的实验验证表明，与现有方法相比，我们的方法在mAP和NDS指标上分别提升了2.93%和2.42%。VR-LRFusion在动态目标检测中表现出鲁棒的检测性能。同时，我们构建了一个煤矿井下环境数据集，并且我们的算法已展现出对该环境的出色适应性。

## 面向自动驾驶感知系统的精度与实时性感知4D雷达预处理

原英文标题：Accuracy- and Real-Time-Aware 4D Radar Preprocessing for Autonomous Driving Perception Systems

作者：Woo-Jin Jung, Dong-Hee Paek, Jeong-Su Park, Seung-Hyun Kong

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-16

原英文摘要：4D radar has emerged as a promising next-generation sensor for improving the robustness of autonomous driving perception systems because of its stable sensing capability under adverse weather conditions. However, deploying 4D radar in embedded environments with limited hardware resources requires radar-representation preprocessing that jointly considers perception accuracy, real-time performance, and computational complexity. This paper proposes a preprocessing framework for 4D-radar-based 3D object detection. First, Percentile-based 3D Shape Preservation (P3DP) extracts point clouds from radar tensors while preserving object-shape information and suppressing noise and false alarms. Second, Multi-frame-based Noise Point Discrimination using Kernel Density Estimation (MF-KDE) improves the density and reliability of sparse radar point clouds. Finally, Embedded \& NetScore (ENS) evaluates suitability for embedded deployment by jointly considering accuracy, real-time performance, adverse-weather robustness, and model complexity.

中文摘要：4D 雷达因其在恶劣天气条件下稳定的感知能力，已成为一种有前景的下一代传感器，用于提高自动驾驶感知系统的鲁棒性。然而，在硬件资源有限的嵌入式环境中部署 4D 雷达，需要同时考虑感知精度、实时性能和计算复杂度的雷达表征预处理。本文提出了一种用于基于 4D 雷达的 3D 目标检测的预处理框架。首先，Percentile-based 3D Shape Preservation (P3DP) 从雷达张量中提取点云，同时保留目标形状信息并抑制噪声和虚警。其次，Multi-frame-based Noise Point Discrimination using Kernel Density Estimation (MF-KDE) 提高了稀疏雷达点云的密度和可靠性。最后，Embedded \& NetScore (ENS) 通过同时考虑精度、实时性能、恶劣天气鲁棒性和模型复杂度，评估嵌入式部署的适用性。

## ARM-SSM：用于高分辨率无线环境重建的空洞无线电地图状态空间模型

原英文标题：ARM-SSM: Atrous Radio Map State Space Models for High-Resolution Wireless Environment Reconstruction

作者：Qiming Zhang, Nan Cheng, Ruixin Zhao, Xiucheng Wang, Haixia Peng, Ruijin Sun

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Network Science and Engineering

出版时间：2026-09-17

原英文摘要：Real-time construction of high-resolution 2D path-loss radio maps is essential for emerging 6G services, such as digital twins (DT), localization, and integrated sensing and communication (ISAC). Existing deep learning approaches, however, incur prohibitive computational and memory costs at fine-grained resolutions. To address this, we propose ARM-SSM (Atrous Radio Map State Space Models), a propagation-motivated framework that leverages the Efficient 2D Scanning (ES2D) mechanism with atrous sampling to exploit the spatial sparsity of wireless propagation. ES2D replaces redundant dense scanning with sparse sampling, maintaining a global receptive field while reducing computational overhead. We validate ARM-SSM on the standard RadioMapSeer benchmark and introduce RM6G-HR (High-Resolution 6G Radio Map dataset), a new large-scale benchmark for high-resolution evaluation. Experiments demonstrate state-of-the-art performance: on RadioMapSeer, ARM-SSM achieves an 8.0% reduction in NMSE compared to leading baselines. Importantly, at $1024 \times 1024$ resolution, it delivers a $1.70\times$ inference speedup relative to RadioMamba while simultaneously reducing NMSE by 8.5%, breaking the accuracy–efficiency trade-off. These results establish ARM-SSM as a scalable and efficient solution for real-time, high-fidelity DTs in next-generation wireless networks.

中文摘要：高分辨率二维路径损耗无线电地图的实时构建对新兴6G服务至关重要，例如数字孪生（DT）、定位以及通感一体化（ISAC）。然而，现有深度学习方法在细粒度分辨率下会带来难以承受的计算和内存开销。为解决这一问题，我们提出ARM-SSM（Atrous Radio Map State Space Models），一种受传播启发的框架，它利用带空洞采样的高效二维扫描（ES2D）机制来利用无线传播的空间稀疏性。ES2D用稀疏采样取代冗余的密集扫描，在保持全局感受野的同时降低计算开销。我们在标准RadioMapSeer基准上验证ARM-SSM，并引入RM6G-HR（高分辨率6G无线电地图数据集），一个用于高分辨率评估的新的大规模基准。实验展示了最先进的性能：在RadioMapSeer上，与领先基线相比，ARM-SSM实现了NMSE降低8.0%。重要的是，在1024 × 1024分辨率下，相对于RadioMamba，它实现了1.70倍的推理加速，同时将NMSE降低8.5%，打破了精度-效率权衡。这些结果确立了ARM-SSM作为面向下一代无线网络中实时、高保真DT的可扩展且高效解决方案。

## DCG-Fixer：用于雷达心跳信号插补、异常检测与修复的深度学习方法

原英文摘要：Doppler Cardiogram (DCG) signals, obtained through radar technology, provide a non-invasive method for vital sign monitoring. However, these signals are often compromised by missing data and anomalies that affect their reliability. This paper presents DCG-Fixer, a novel self-supervised deep learning framework designed to address these challenges. DCG-Fixer employs a two-stage quality assessment, Q V I and Q H E R , to ensure the selection of high-quality segments for training. DCG-Fixer’s architecture, DCG-Fix-Net, combines temporal and frequency-domain processing streams for signal restoration. ## 旋转雷达多普勒速度测量能否改进车辆检测与跟踪？

原英文标题：Do Spinning Radar Doppler Velocity Measurements Improve Vehicle Detection and Tracking?

作者：Eric Xie, Daniil Lisus, Timothy D. Barfoot

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-17

原英文摘要：Spinning frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radars have been gaining popularity in autonomous vehicle perception on account of their robustness to adverse weather conditions and 360° field of view. Recently, scanning radars have also been shown capable of generating per-azimuth Doppler velocity. In this paper, we investigate whether these Doppler velocity measurements improve spinning radar vehicle detection and tracking performance. For detection, we estimate the ego motion and use it to undo the Doppler range distortion of the radar image before passing it to a network. For tracking, we propose a new way to estimate a per-vehicle velocity and use it as a prior for the tracker's motion model. Since Doppler-enabled spinning radar data is not available in any dataset with ground-truth dynamic object labels, our first contribution is an automatic labelling pipeline that uses an ensemble of fine-tuned off-the-shelf lidar detectors to label all 643 km of the Boreas Road Trip dataset. We then transfer detections to radar, and use over 250 km of vehicle-dense sequences as ground-truth training data. By training and evaluating two state-of-the-art detectors, we show that Doppler undistortion can improve detection accuracy by up to $2.37$ points on mean average precision. Furthermore, we show that the Doppler velocity prior can improve tracking accuracy by $13.68$ points on multi-object tracking accuracy (MOTA) versus the zero-velocity initialization baseline, while achieving $99.7\%$ of the MOTA obtained using ground-truth velocities as the prior.

中文摘要：旋转式调频连续波（FMCW）雷达因其对恶劣天气条件的鲁棒性和 360° 视场，在自动驾驶车辆感知中越来越受欢迎。最近，扫描雷达也被证明能够生成逐方位角的多普勒速度。在本文中，我们研究这些多普勒速度测量是否改善旋转式雷达车辆检测与跟踪性能。对于检测，我们估计自车运动，并在将雷达图像传入网络之前，用它来消除雷达图像的多普勒距离畸变。对于跟踪，我们提出一种新方法来估计每辆车的速度，并将其用作跟踪器运动模型的先验。由于在任何具有真值动态物体标签的数据集中都没有可用的支持多普勒的旋转式雷达数据，我们的第一项贡献是一个自动标注流程，它使用一组微调过的现成激光雷达检测器来标注 Boreas Road Trip 数据集的全部 643 km。然后我们将检测结果迁移到雷达，并使用超过 250 km 的车辆密集序列作为真值训练数据。通过训练和评估两个最先进的检测器，我们表明多普勒去畸变可以在平均精度均值上最多提高检测精度 $2.37$ 个点。此外，我们表明，与零速度初始化基线相比，多普勒速度先验可以在多目标跟踪精度（MOTA）上提高跟踪精度 $13.68$ 个点，同时达到使用真值速度作为先验所获得的 MOTA 的 $99.7\%$。

## 基于多尺度特征学习的域泛化毫米波雷达手势识别

原英文标题：Domain-generalized millimeter-wave radar gesture recognition via multi-scale feature learning

作者：Yichun Chen, Zhimeng Xu, Huiling Chen, Liangqin Chen, Zhizhang David Chen

出版来源：Physical Communication

出版时间：2026-09-18

原英文摘要：Millimeter-wave radar provides a contactless sensing modality for gesture recognition in wireless intelligent systems, yet recognition accuracy can degrade sharply when users and environments differ from those seen during training. The difficulty arises from background clutter, multipath reflections, body micro-motion, and individual motion patterns, which alter the distribution of radar echoes under limited source-domain data. This paper presents a domain-generalized mmWave radar gesture recognition framework that integrates interference-aware preprocessing with multi-scale feature learning. A Human Micro-Motion Filtering (HMMF) method first suppresses quasi-static body responses and environmental clutter while retaining gesture-related Doppler and angular information. Doppler-time and angle-time representations are then combined and processed by a Multi-Level Feature Fusion ConvNeXt network to learn discriminative radar features across complementary scales. A hybrid objective with contrastive learning further regularizes the feature space by improving intra-class compactness and inter-class separation. Experiments on the public MCD-Gesture Dataset show that the method achieves 96.28% accuracy when tested on unseen users and environments, indicating its robustness for radar-based contactless gesture sensing.

中文摘要：毫米波雷达为无线智能系统中的手势识别提供了一种非接触式传感模态，然而当用户和环境与训练时所见不同时，识别准确率可能急剧下降。该困难源于背景杂波、多径反射、身体微动和个体运动模式，这些因素会在源域数据有限的情况下改变雷达回波的分布。本文提出了一种域泛化的毫米波雷达手势识别框架，该框架将干扰感知预处理与多尺度特征学习相结合。一种人体微动滤波（Human Micro-Motion Filtering, HMMF）方法首先抑制准静态身体响应和环境杂波，同时保留与手势相关的多普勒和角度信息。随后，多普勒-时间与角度-时间表示被结合，并由多级特征融合 ConvNeXt 网络处理，以学习跨互补尺度的判别性雷达特征。结合对比学习的混合目标通过提高类内紧凑性和类间分离度进一步正则化特征空间。在公开 MCD-Gesture 数据集上的实验表明，该方法在未见用户和环境下测试时达到 96.28% 的准确率，表明其对于基于雷达的非接触式手势感测具有鲁棒性。

## 别窥探我的私人空间：利用 mmWave 传感器为玻璃墙区域提供隐私保护

作者：Asterios Parlitsis, Girish Vaidya, Marco Zuniga

出版来源：Proceedings of the 2026 International Conference on Embedded Wireless Systems and Networks (EWSN)

出版时间：2026-09-17

原英文摘要：Glass walls reduce illumination costs, enhance thermal comfort and improve social safety. However, they raise privacy concerns. People do not like passersby looking into personal areas, such as computer monitors. Our solution combines two upcoming technologies, mmWave sensors and transparent screens. The mmWave sensors detect passersby, and the transparent screen allows us to turn opaque only a small area instead of the entire wall. We build upon studies performing human pose estimation (HPE) from radar point clouds while addressing their two important shortcomings. They (i) focus on single static users, and (ii) estimate only the right pose, not the exact body location. Our live evaluation shows that, for three passersby, dynamic opaque areas of 30 cm × 30 cm block the line of sight over 95% of the time.
