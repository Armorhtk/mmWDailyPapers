## 基于 JEPA 的场层世界模型：桥接信道预测与估计 [Preprint]

原英文标题：A JEPA-Based Field-Layer World Model for Bridging Channel Prediction and Estimation

作者：Yuzhi Yang, Brahim Mefgouda, Hang Zou, Lina Bariah, Anis Bara, Yuhuan Lu, Hao Zhang, MérouaneDebbah

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-08-10

原英文摘要：Channel state information (CSI) acquisition, reconstruction, and prediction are fundamental yet costly tasks in modern MIMO-OFDM wireless systems. Direct coefficient-level prediction of raw CSI is fragile in realistic propagation environments, since small spatial perturbations, local scattering changes, and phase variations can cause large errors in the complex channel domain. However, the underlying wireless propagation field still contains stable and predictable structures that can be exploited across time, frequency, antenna, and carrier dimensions. Motivated by this observation, we propose a JEPA-based field-layer world model (FWM) that learns a shared latent propagation state from multi-resolution CSI observations across the considered carrier bands and predicts its task-relevant evolution in the latent domain. The proposed FWM maps multiple CSI observation resolutions to a shared latent propagation-field space through scale-specific tokenizer heads. A latent prediction backbone is then trained to infer masked or future field states, while an incremental multi-scale alignment strategy allows new observation scales to be incorporated without retraining the entire model from scratch. For downstream reconstruction, the predicted latent field is used as a structured prior and combined with sparse current pilots. Experiments on single-band and cross-band reconstruction demonstrate improved symbol detection and, more notably, substantial beamforming gains despite modest NMSE improvements, indicating that FWM captures task-relevant spatial propagation structure beyond coefficient-wise CSI fitting.

中文摘要：在现代 MIMO-OFDM 无线系统中，信道状态信息（CSI）的获取、重建和预测是基础但成本高昂的任务。在真实传播环境中，对原始 CSI 的直接系数级预测是脆弱的，因为小的空间扰动、局部散射变化和相位变化会在复信道域中导致较大误差。然而，潜在无线传播场仍包含稳定的、可预测的结构，这些结构可以在时间、频率、天线和载波维度上加以利用。基于这一观察，我们提出了一种基于 JEPA 的场层世界模型（FWM），该模型从所考虑载波频段上的多分辨率 CSI 观测中学习共享的潜在传播状态，并在潜在域中预测其与任务相关的演化。所提出的 FWM 通过尺度特定的 tokenizer 头将多个 CSI 观测分辨率映射到共享的潜在传播场空间。随后训练一个潜在预测主干来推断被掩蔽或未来的场状态，同时一种增量式多尺度对齐策略允许在不从头重新训练整个模型的情况下纳入新的观测尺度。对于下游重建，预测的潜在场被用作结构化先验，并与稀疏的当前导频相结合。在单频段和跨频段重建上的实验表明，尽管 NMSE 改善幅度不大，但符号检测性能提升显著，且波束成形增益尤为可观，这说明 FWM 捕获了超出逐系数 CSI 拟合的任务相关空间传播结构。

## TA-VMRNN：基于FMCW雷达的实时手势识别系统 [J]

原英文标题：A Real-Time FMCW Radar Hand Gesture Recognition System Based on TA-VMRNN

作者：Li Che, Junbi Yang, Liubing Jiang, Haixin Yang

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Journal

出版时间：2026-08-10

原英文摘要：To address the limitations of vision-based gesture recognition regarding illumination dependence and privacy concerns, as well as the parameter redundancy of traditional radar deep learning models, this paper proposes a real-time frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar gesture recognition system based on a lightweight TA-VMRNN architecture. Range, Doppler, and Angle features are extracted from radar signals to construct Range-Time (RTM), Doppler-Time (DTM), and Angle-Time (ATM) maps as network inputs. In terms of feature processing, the proposed TA-VMRNN architecture adopts Triplet Attention at the front end and incorporates a VMRNN spatiotemporal memory network at the back end to achieve radar feature extraction and fusion. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed model achieves a recognition accuracy of 99.24% on a handwritten gesture dataset containing digits 0-9, while the number of parameters (Params) and floating-point operations (FLOPs) are only 0.465 M and 0.056 G, respectively. Furthermore, in leave-one-subject-out (LOSO) cross-validation, the average accuracies for offline and real-time recognition reach 97.62% and 97.37%, respectively. In a discrete grid spatial test, the system obtains an average recognition accuracy of 96.45%. The proposed end-to-end system provides a highly viable and practical solution for human-computer interaction scenarios under constrained computing resources.

中文摘要：为解决基于视觉的手势识别在光照依赖和隐私方面的局限性，以及传统雷达深度学习模型的参数冗余问题，本文提出了一种基于轻量级TA-VMRNN架构的实时调频连续波（FMCW）雷达手势识别系统。从雷达信号中提取距离、多普勒和角度特征，构建距离-时间图（RTM）、多普勒-时间图（DTM）和角度-时间图（ATM）作为网络输入。在特征处理方面，所提出的TA-VMRNN架构前端采用三元组注意力（Triplet Attention），后端引入VMRNN时空记忆网络，实现雷达特征提取与融合。实验结果表明，该模型在包含数字0-9的手写手势数据集上识别准确率达到99.24%，而参数量（Params）和浮点运算量（FLOPs）分别仅为0.465 M和0.056 G。此外，在留一受试者（LOSO）交叉验证中，离线识别和实时识别的平均准确率分别达到97.62%和97.37%。在离散网格空间测试中，系统获得96.45%的平均识别准确率。所提出的端到端系统为计算资源受限条件下的人机交互场景提供了一种高度可行且实用的解决方案。

## 一种鲁棒的仿真-实测跨域小样本HRRP目标识别算法 [J]

原英文标题：A Robust Algorithm for Simulation‐Measured Cross‐Domain Few‐Shot HRRP Target Recognition

作者：Xiaoyu Xu, Mingbo Zhu, Keyuan Yu, Kangsheng Liu

出版来源：Electronics Letters

出版时间：2026-08-05

原英文摘要：ABSTRACT Aiming at the scarcity of labelled measured high‐resolution range profile (HRRP) samples for non‐cooperative targets and severe distribution discrepancy between simulated and real echoes, this paper proposes CROF‐Net, a robust cross‐domain few‐shot recognition framework adapted to complex electromagnetic environments. First, low‐order moment matching is adopted to calibrate the distribution of simulated data and measured samples are enhanced to improve domain consistency. A 1D‐conformer backbone integrated with instance normalisation and scattering‐peak‐aware modules is constructed to extract robust local and global features from distorted HRRP signals. To address the intra‐class multimodal distribution caused by target attitudes, we establish an orthogonal‐constrained sub‐centre ArcFace metric space, which widens classification boundaries and mitigates negative transfer as well as catastrophic forgetting. Experimental results on the SAMPLE and MTDSP datasets demonstrate that CROF‐Net outperforms in recognition accuracy and anti‐interference capability and it possesses promising research value and engineering application prospects.

作者：Kailu Guo, Elif Dogu, Khalid Z. Rajab

出版来源：Scientific Data

出版时间：2026-08-10

原英文摘要：Abstract This work presents a controlled millimetre-wave radar point-cloud dataset for analysing guided daily and rehabilitation-related movements in standardised indoor settings. The dataset is designed to support radar-based human motion analysis: radar point clouds serve as the primary sensing modality, while the Azure Kinect provides 3D skeleton labels for supervised learning and validation. Data were collected from 26 healthy, able-bodied adults aged 25–57 years. Participants followed reference videos to perform a set of daily and rehabilitation-related movements under two visual conditions: normal lighting and low-light settings. After quality control, the release contains approximately 3.47 minutes of retained movement data per participant per lighting condition. The original recording-level data include 265,487 radar frames and 322,833 Kinect-derived skeleton-label frames, while the processed aligned data contain 137,717 paired radar–skeleton frames. Each aligned radar frame is annotated with an action label, subject ID, and corresponding Kinect-derived 3D skeleton. By combining radar point clouds with aligned skeleton labels across controlled lighting conditions, the dataset supports tasks such as radar-based action classification, identity recognition, and keypoint detection.

## A-LOAF：基于AOA-FMCW估计的实时声学定位框架 [J]

原英文标题：A-LOAF: A Real-Time Acoustic Localization Framework Based on AOA-FMCW Estimation

作者：Guangyao Liu, Yuzhang Xi, Weimeng Cui, Bo Yin, Xinwei Gao, Liu Yang, Shuyu Li, He Kong, Zhi Wang

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement

出版时间：2026-08-06

原英文摘要：Accurate and stable position and orientation estimation in indoor environments is critical for many real-world applications, yet remains an open challenge without a universally optimal solution. In this work, we propose A-LOAF, a novel acoustic-based system that achieves real-time and precise indoor localization and orientation estimation using commercial speakers and a compact microphone array. The system employs spatially distributed acoustic sources to emit inaudible near-ultrasonic linear frequency-modulated (LFM) signals, which are captured by a microphone array mounted on the mobile device to be localized. An angle-of-arrival (AOA) estimation algorithm is employed to determine source directions in real time, and device pose is jointly estimated by minimizing an AOA-related objective function. To further enhance localization accuracy, frequency-modulated continuous wave (FMCW) ranging is integrated to compute the relative displacement between keyframes as interframe constraints, supported by a dedicated keyframe selection strategy. The FMCW-based constraint is then incorporated as a penalty term into the AOA-based optimization framework, enabling robust and high-precision pose estimation. Experimental results in complex real-world indoor environments demonstrate that A-LOAF achieves a median localization error of 0.1171 m and a median orientation error of 0.60°, with an average computational time of 0.0884 s per frame, confirming its effectiveness and practical applicability. To benefit the community, we make the source code publicly available at https://github.com/zjuersdsd/ALOAF.git

## 面向毫米波雷达的动作语义引导的Z轴点云重建 [J]

原英文标题：Action-Semantics-Guided Z -Axis Point Cloud Reconstruction for Millimeter-Wave Radar

作者：Wei Yin, Ling-Feng Shi, Yifan Shi

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement

出版时间：2026-08-06

原英文摘要：In industrial indoor settings, mmWave radar often yields unstable $Z$ -axis (height) estimates because the pitch-direction virtual aperture is limited. This produces semi-ring/band-likeZdistortions and flattened point clouds, obscuring human structure and blurring limb contours, which degrades readability and downstream interpretation. The root cause is that sparse pitch virtual elements compress pitch information into tiny phase differences, making height reconstruction sensitive to noise, multipath, and calibration errors; small pitch jitter is then amplified into pronounced $Z$ -axis curvature in 3-D imaging. We propose Action-guided $Z$ -axis Reconstruction Network (ActZ-Net), which incorporates action-category priors for $Z$ -axis geometric reconstruction to restore clearer and more interpretable human 3-D structures from sparse mmWave radar point clouds with severe $Z$ -axis distortion. ActZ-Net comprises three components: 1) action-semantically adaptive normalization (Ada-ActNorm) enables differentiated activation of geometric channels across different actions, 2) $Z$ -axis anisotropic attention explicitly incorporating thezcoordinate to enhance height modelling, and 3) offset regression based on a reference point set to avoid geometric drift caused by direct coordinate generation. Experiments demonstrate that compared to methods such as mmDEAR and mmBaT, ActZ-Net reduces chamfer distance (CD) to 0.18, and increases point retention ratio (PRR) to 0.90 compared to mmDEAR and mmBaT, achieving 0.12 and 0.11 on Z-range error and Z-variance consistency (ZRE/ZVC) metrics, improving $Z$ -axis structural integrity and visual readability.

中文摘要：在工业室内环境中，毫米波（mmWave）雷达因俯仰方向虚拟孔径有限，常产生不稳定的Z轴（高度）估计。这导致半环/带状Z畸变和扁平化点云，模糊人体结构并虚化肢体轮廓，降低了可读性和后续解译质量。根本原因是稀疏的俯仰虚拟阵元将俯仰信息压缩为微小相位差，使高度重建对噪声、多路径和标定误差敏感；微小的俯仰抖动随后在三维成像中被放大为明显的Z轴弯曲。我们提出了动作引导的Z轴重建网络（ActZ-Net），该网络将动作类别先验融入Z轴几何重建，从具有严重Z轴畸变的稀疏毫米波雷达点云中恢复更清晰、更可解释的人体三维结构。ActZ-Net 包含三个组成部分：1）动作语义自适应归一化（Ada-ActNorm），对不同动作实现几何通道的差异化激活；2）Z轴各向异性注意力，显式引入z坐标以增强高度建模；3）基于参考点集的偏移回归，避免直接坐标生成导致的几何漂移。实验表明，与mmDEAR和mmBaT等方法相比，ActZ-Net将倒角距离（CD）降至0.18，点保留率（PRR）提升至0.90；在Z范围误差和Z方差一致性（ZRE/ZVC）指标上分别达到0.12和0.11，改善了Z轴结构完整性和视觉可读性。

## 基于锚点的AI方法：利用汽车雷达微多普勒特征进行碰撞前目标检测 [Preprint]

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-08-09

原英文摘要：Advanced automated driving presents significant potential to improve modern automotive safety systems, but it depends highly on the reliable activation of restraint systems. Forward-looking sensors are crucial for immediate and precise object detection. Recent developments in automotive radar technology enable detailed environment detection and the recognition of high-resolution features, such as micro-Doppler signatures. Combined with advanced AI techniques, these features significantly enhance object detection and improve the accuracy of kinematic parameter estimation. This is essential for the early and reliable activation of irreversible safety systems, such as smart airbags and adaptive seat belts. Therefore, an anchor-based AI model is presented, designed to process high-resolution radar data with an explicit focus on micro-Doppler signatures to improve pre-crash object detection. Furthermore, these signatures can improve the accuracy of kinematic object parameter estimation and reduce false negatives, especially in the critical near-field. To address the challenges of sparse and fluctuating radar point clouds, an innovative radar-image dilation technique on the feature input channels was developed to amplify local radar patterns, like micro-Doppler features. Therefore, this approach increases the system's reliability and increases its ability to detect objects in pre-crash scenarios despite radar multipath reflections and ghost objects. In order to investigate the applicability and compare the model's performance with advanced automotive radar tracking methods, a radar data set using series sensors and pre-crash relevant scenarios was recorded. The results demonstrate the advantages of the anchor-based AI model over established tracking approaches. It excels at estimating object parameters in dynamic scenarios and underscores its ability to process different data sets effectively.

中文摘要：先进的自动驾驶在提升现代汽车安全系统方面具有巨大潜力，但其高度依赖于约束系统的可靠触发。前向传感器对于即时且精确的目标检测至关重要。汽车雷达技术的最新发展使得详细的环境检测和高分辨率特征（如微多普勒特征）的识别成为可能。结合先进的AI技术，这些特征显著增强了目标检测能力，并提高了运动学参数估计的准确性。这对于早期可靠地触发不可逆安全系统（如智能安全气囊和自适应安全带）至关重要。为此，提出了一种基于锚点的AI模型，该模型专门处理高分辨率雷达数据，并明确关注微多普勒特征，以改善碰撞前目标检测。此外，这些特征可以提高运动学目标参数估计的准确性，并减少漏检，尤其是在关键近场区域。为了解决雷达点云稀疏且波动的问题，开发了一种在特征输入通道上创新的雷达图像扩张技术，以增强局部雷达模式，如微多普勒特征。因此，该方法提高了系统的可靠性，并增强了在碰撞前场景中即便存在雷达多径反射和虚假目标的情况下检测目标的能力。为了研究该模型的适用性并将其性能与先进的汽车雷达跟踪方法进行比较，使用量产传感器记录了一个包含碰撞前相关场景的雷达数据集。结果表明，基于锚点的AI模型相较于已建立的跟踪方法具有优势。它在动态场景中估计目标参数方面表现出色，并凸显了其有效处理不同数据集的能力。

## 二进制相位调制FMCW雷达用于高性能多人心脏诱导胸廓微动传感 [J]

原英文标题：Binary Phase Modulation FMCW Radar for High-Performance Multiperson Cardiac-Induced Thoracic Micro-Motion Sensing

作者：Jingyun Lu, Jiayu Zhang, Yuchen Li, Changzhan Gu

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques

出版时间：2026-08-07

原英文摘要：This article focuses on the exploitation of binary phase modulation (BPM) radar architecture for multiperson fine-grained cardiac-induced thoracic micro-motion monitoring. The BPM scheme is developed based on a conventional frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar framework. By introducing a phase shifter at each transmit (TX) channel, all TX channels can operate simultaneously while preserving temporal resolution. Compared with conventional radar architectures, such as phased-array and time-division multiplexing (TDM) MIMO radar, the proposed scheme provides improved signal-to-noise ratio ( $SNR$ ), making it suitable for noncontact biomedical sensing applications. A rigorous mathematical analysis of the BPM operating principle is presented in the context of vital sign extraction, with particular emphasis on the impact of practical imperfections, such as phase errors and amplitude mismatches, on displacement demodulation.
