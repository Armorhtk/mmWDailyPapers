## UAV 天底方向的 3D 雷达成像

原英文标题：3D Radar Imaging From the UAV Nadir

作者：S. Hamed Javadi, Hichem Sahli, André Bourdoux

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-04

原英文摘要：Radars improve the sensing robustness of UAVs by operating under poor lighting and weather conditions and seeing through occlusions such as vegetation. However, they suffer from poor angular resolution, which can be addressed using synthetic aperture radar (SAR) algorithms. State-of-the-art UAV SAR methods operate at a depression angle and are not suitable for sensor fusion applications where the data are collected from areas directly below the UAV (i.e., the UAV nadir). In this paper, we present an interferometric SAR (InSAR) framework for reconstructing 3D images from the UAV nadir using a low-cost multi-input-multi-output (MIMO) mm-wave radar. Additionally, an effective method based on the phase gradient autofocus (PGA) is presented for compensating the phase error across the virtual receive antennas. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our 3D imaging algorithm in both simulation and experimental scenarios.

中文摘要：雷达通过在恶劣光照和天气条件下运行以及穿透植被等遮挡物进行观测，提高了无人机的感知鲁棒性。然而，它们存在角分辨率差的问题，这可以通过合成孔径雷达（SAR）算法来解决。最先进的无人机 SAR 方法工作在俯角下，不适用于数据从无人机正下方区域（即无人机天底）采集的传感器融合应用。在本文中，我们提出了一种干涉合成孔径雷达（InSAR）框架，用于使用低成本多输入多输出（MIMO）毫米波雷达从无人机天底重建 3D 图像。此外，提出了一种基于相位梯度自聚焦（PGA）的有效方法，用于补偿跨虚拟接收天线的相位误差。我们在仿真和实验场景中证明了我们的 3D 成像算法的有效性。

## 使用MMW MIMO天线阵列系统的快速3-D近场成像方法

原英文标题：A Fast 3-D Near-Field Imaging Method Using MMW MIMO Antenna Array System

作者：Guanghui Pang, Jian Zhang, Tao Su, Jiangtao Liu, Shaohong Xue, Ze Hu

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement

出版时间：2026-09-22

原英文摘要：Large-scale millimeter-wave (MMW) multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) arrays provide high-resolution near-field three-dimensional (3-D) imaging but suffer from severe computational burdens due to the massive volume of raw echo data. To address this critical bottleneck, this article proposes a highly efficient two-stage imaging framework. In the first stage, an equivalent phase center (EPC)-based multistatic-to-monostatic conversion (MMC) compressively reduces the data dimensionality, enabling a 3-D range migration algorithm (RMA) to rapidly generate a coarse global image. In the second stage, the scene is dynamically partitioned, and predefined thresholds are applied to extract target blocks. Subsequently, a lightweight 2-D holographic slice reconstruction (HSR) algorithm is executed exclusively on these localized blocks to achieve refined high-resolution imaging. Furthermore, the impact of transceiver separation on imaging quality is analytically evaluated, and the optimal block size is derived to maximize the well-focused region while minimizing computational overhead. Validated through simulations and a custom-integrated 512×512 MMW MIMO hardware prototype, the proposed framework achieves comparable imaging quality to conventional algorithms yet delivers orders-of-magnitude reduction in execution time, thereby offering a highly practical solution for real-time near-field MMW sensing applications.

中文摘要：大规模毫米波（MMW）多输入多输出（MIMO）阵列可提供高分辨率近场三维（3-D）成像，但由于原始回波数据量巨大，面临严重的计算负担。为解决这一关键瓶颈，本文提出一种高效的两阶段成像框架。在第一阶段，基于等效相位中心（EPC）的多基地到单基地转换（MMC）以压缩方式降低数据维度，使三维距离迁移算法（RMA）能够快速生成粗略全局图像。在第二阶段，对场景进行动态分区，并应用预定义阈值提取目标块。随后，仅在这些局部块上执行轻量级二维（2-D）全息切片重建（HSR）算法，以实现精细高分辨率成像。此外，对收发器分离对成像质量的影响进行了解析评估，并推导出最优块大小，以在最小化计算开销的同时最大化良好聚焦区域。通过仿真和定制集成的512×512 MMW MIMO硬件原型验证，所提框架实现了与传统算法相当的成像质量，却将执行时间降低数个数量级，从而为实时近场MMW感知应用提供一种高度实用的解决方案。

## 演示：材质感知RF数字孪生：无需真实世界标签的mmWave目标识别

原英文标题：Demo: A Material-Aware RF Digital Twin for mmWave Object Recognition with No Real-World Labels

作者：Emily Bejerano, Federico Tondolo, Ayaan Qayyum, Xiaofan Yu, Xiaofan Jiang

出版来源：Proceedings of the ACM Workshop on Digital Twins over NextG Wireless Networks

出版时间：2026-10-26

原英文摘要：Millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar perception is bottlenecked by training data: every room reflects mmWave differently, so models do not transfer and labeling radar in each new space is impractical. We demonstrate a material-aware RF digital twin for object recognition with no real radar labels. From a 3D scan the twin infers per-surface electromagnetic materials and ray-traces frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar, and because the scene is known, each synthetic signature is automatically labeled and supervision is free. Our key finding: matching a simulated signal to real radar fails, but a scale-free geometry factor, shape read from how the reflection modulates over one rotation, transfers, because it divides out the absolute terms that do not survive the sim-to-real gap. In the live demo, attendees place household objects on a turntable before a real 77 GHz radar and the factor separates them by shape class, symmetric versus irregular, not by instance identity.

中文摘要：毫米波（mmWave）雷达感知受限于训练数据：每个房间对毫米波的反射都不同，因此模型无法迁移，而在每个新空间中标注雷达数据又不切实际。我们展示了一个材料感知的射频（RF）数字孪生，用于在没有任何真实雷达标签的情况下进行物体识别。该孪生从 3D 扫描中推断逐表面的电磁材料，并对调频连续波（FMCW）雷达进行射线追踪；由于场景已知，每个合成信号特征都被自动标注，监督是免费的。我们的关键发现是：将仿真信号与真实雷达进行匹配会失败，但一个尺度无关的几何因子——从反射在一次旋转中如何调制读取出的形状——可以迁移，因为它约掉了那些无法在仿真到现实差距中存活的绝对项。在现场演示中，参会者将家用物体放在转台上，置于真实的 77 GHz 雷达前，而该因子按形状类别（对称与不规则）将它们区分开，而不是按实例身份。

出版来源：Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence

出版时间：2026-09-25

原英文摘要：Continuous monitoring of eye-blink activity provides a non-contact behavioral indicator relevant to fatigue-related applications and human-state monitoring. Reliable radar-based sensing remains difficult under the tested single-user office-like desktop conditions because blink-induced eyelid motion is weak, short-lived, and easily contaminated by respiration, head motion, and transient clutter. This study proposes a physics-regularized, reliability-aware artificial intelligence framework for 77-GHz millimeter-wave radar that combines adaptive variational mode decomposition with the Multi-modal Adaptive Deep Network (MAD-Net). Time-domain phase and frequency-domain representations are processed in parallel, aligned, fused, and temporally modeled for blink recognition. Doppler bandwidth is used only as a training-time supervisory signal for physics regularization and is not required during inference. Under subject-level macro-averaging across the 30 outer leave-one-subject-out folds on the retained labeled-window evaluation set, the proposed framework achieves 92.5% accuracy, 91.0% F1-score, and a 1.5% window-level false-positive rate, with an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve of 0.943. The results support non-contact blink monitoring under the tested single-user office-like desktop conditions.

中文摘要：连续监测眨眼活动提供了一种非接触式行为指标，该指标与疲劳相关应用和人体状态监测相关。在所测试的单用户类办公桌面条件下，可靠的基于雷达的传感仍然困难，因为眨眼引起的眼睑运动微弱、短暂，并且容易受到呼吸、头部运动和瞬态杂波的污染。本研究提出了一种用于77 GHz毫米波雷达的物理正则化、可靠性感知人工智能框架，该框架将自适应变分模态分解与多模态自适应深度网络（MAD-Net）相结合。时域相位表示和频域表示被并行处理、对齐、融合，并针对眨眼识别进行时间建模。多普勒带宽仅用作物理正则化的训练时监督信号，在推理期间不需要。在保留的带标签窗口评估集上，跨30个外层留一受试者折进行受试者级宏平均的情况下，所提框架达到92.5%的准确率、91.0%的F1分数和1.5%的窗口级假阳性率，受试者工作特征曲线下面积为0.943。这些结果支持在所测试的单用户类办公桌面条件下进行非接触式眨眼监测。

## 用于生命体征监测中CW Doppler雷达主动定位的信号质量指数

原英文标题：A Signal Quality Index for Active Positioning of CW Doppler Radar in Vital-Sign Monitoring

作者：Huu-Son Nguyen, Masaki Kurosawa, Hoang Thi Yen, Takuya Hashimoto, Cong-Kha Pham, Guanghao Sun

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Letters

出版时间：2026-09-24

原英文摘要：Continuous-wave (CW) Doppler radar for vital-sign monitoring loses sensitivity periodically at unbalanced operating points, only $\lambda /8$ from the nearest balanced point, with no reference-free way to detect the drop or search for a fix. This Letter introduces a Signal Quality Index (SQI): the triplet of signal magnitude ( $M$ ), I/Q amplitude ratio ( $R$ ), and midpoint phase angle ( $\theta _{\text{mid}}$ ), each computed from one oscillation cycle of I/Q baseband, which quantifies the operating state at the current position alone. A blind two-stage active-sensing pipeline then uses the SQI to search without prior spatial knowledge: gradient ascent on $M$ locates the high-quality zone, and a systematic scan within it finds SQI-balanced positions. Across 1705 offline trials replaying bench data (341 positions × 5 chest-wall displacement amplitudes), the pipeline reaches the high-quality zone in 87.0% of trials, finds a first balanced candidate within a median of 25–26 steps, and identifies a balanced point in 98.2–100% of trials.

中文摘要：用于生命体征监测的连续波（CW）多普勒雷达在不平衡工作点处会周期性地损失灵敏度，这些工作点距最近平衡点仅 λ/8，且没有无参考方法来检测这种下降或搜索修复方案。本文引入信号质量指数（SQI）：由信号幅度（M）、I/Q 幅度比（R）和中点相位角（θ_mid）组成的三元组，每个量均由 I/Q 基带的一个振荡周期计算得到，仅量化当前位置的工作状态。随后，一个盲式两阶段主动感知流程使用 SQI 在无先验空间知识的情况下进行搜索：对 M 的梯度上升定位高质量区域，并在其中进行系统扫描以找到 SQI 平衡位置。在重放台架数据的 1705 次离线试验中（341 个位置 × 5 个胸壁位移幅度），该流程在 87.0% 的试验中到达高质量区域，在 25–26 步的中位数内找到第一个平衡候选位置，并在 98.2–100% 的试验中识别出平衡点。

## AdaSleep：面向腰带与雷达无感睡眠分期的鲁棒无源域自适应框架

原英文标题：AdaSleep: A Robust Source-Free Domain Adaptation Framework for Unobtrusive Sleep Staging via Belt and Radar

作者：Xuan Luo, Zhi Lu, Fang Zhou, Yu Pu, Dongheng Zhang, Yan Chen

出版来源：Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies

出版时间：2026-09-30

原英文摘要：Unobtrusive sleep staging based on respiratory signals enables long-term sleep monitoring, yet real-world deployment is often hindered by domain shifts caused by demographic heterogeneity, environmental noise, and differences in sensing modality. Privacy constraints can make source data inaccessible, motivating Source-Free Domain Adaptation (SFDA), which uses only a source-pretrained model and unlabeled target data. However, existing self-training based SFDA methods are vulnerable to confirmation bias: signal artifacts can induce erroneous pseudo-labels that are subsequently reinforced during adaptation. To address these challenges, we propose AdaSleep, a robust framework for adapting a source-pretrained model using unlabeled target data. AdaSleep introduces a consistency-based self-training strategy built on a Teacher-Student architecture. By aggregating predictions across stochastically augmented views, AdaSleep reduces sensitivity to random perturbations and produces more stable pseudo-labels. It further applies a Quality Gate to restrict model updates with reliable pseudo-labels, thereby reducing error propagation. Extensive evaluations across four real-world datasets spanning abdominal belt and radar sensing in hospital and home environments show that AdaSleep outperforms the compared SFDA baselines, with average improvements of 3.67% in accuracy and 3.90% in Macro-F1 over the source-only baseline. These results support AdaSleep as a reliable adaptation strategy for privacy-sensitive unobtrusive sleep monitoring.

中文摘要：基于呼吸信号的非侵入式睡眠分期能够实现长期睡眠监测，然而实际部署常常受到由人口统计学异质性、环境噪声和传感模态差异引起的域偏移的阻碍。隐私约束可能使源数据不可访问，促使无源域适应（SFDA），其仅使用源预训练模型和未标记目标数据。然而，现有基于自训练的 SFDA 方法容易受到确认偏误的影响：信号伪影可能诱导错误的伪标签，而这些伪标签随后在适应过程中被强化。为应对这些挑战，我们提出 AdaSleep，一个使用未标记目标数据来适应源预训练模型的稳健框架。AdaSleep 引入了一种基于一致性的自训练策略，构建在 Teacher-Student 架构之上。通过在随机增强视图之间聚合预测，AdaSleep 降低了对随机扰动的敏感性，并产生更稳定的伪标签。它进一步应用 Quality Gate 来限制使用可靠伪标签进行模型更新，从而减少错误传播。在跨越腹部绑带和雷达传感、医院和家庭环境的四个真实世界数据集上的广泛评估表明，AdaSleep 优于所比较的 SFDA 基线，在准确率上平均提升 3.67%，在 Macro-F1 上平均提升 3.90%，相比仅源基线。这些结果支持 AdaSleep 作为一种可靠的适应策略，用于隐私敏感的非侵入式睡眠监测。

原英文摘要：In this article, we present a generative method for micro-Doppler signatures to augment the training data of human activity recognition (HAR) systems. An auxiliary classifier-based autoencoder (ACAE) first encodes authentic signatures into a low-dimensional representation space. We then develop a time–frequency causality-based representation generator (TFCRG) to model dependencies among representation elements. Multiple causality blocks (CBs), each with two specialized masks, capture time and frequency dependencies separately. Sampling from the learned distribution allows TFCRG to produce synthetic representations that approximate the distribution of authentic representations. Finally, a representation mix-up strategy combines two encoded representations and their category labels using the same coefficient. TFCRG generates a new representation under this mixed condition, and the ACAE decoder reconstructs a synthetic signature. This strategy increases sample diversity and produces category-ambiguous samples with soft labels for HAR. Extensive experiments on two widely used radar HAR databases demonstrate strong generation quality and downstream HAR gains relative to the compared methods.

原英文摘要：Automotive spinning FMCW radar provides dense, $360^\circ$ sensing and remains reliable under poor illumination and adverse weather, making it well-suited to autonomous navigation. However, heading changes appear as circular shifts in the polar radar representation, and conventional global aggregation can lose relationships among radar responses that are important for distinguishing similar places. ## 超越径向限制：恢复方向不变的行走动态以实现鲁棒mmWave雷达行人重识别

原英文标题：Beyond Radial Limits: Recovering Direction-Invariant Walking Dynamics for Robust mmWave Radar Person Re-Identification

作者：Teng Huang, Han Ding, Cui Zhao, Fei Wang, Ge Wang, Jizhong Zhao, Zhi Wang, Wei Xi

出版来源：Proceedings of the ACM on Interactive, Mobile, Wearable and Ubiquitous Technologies

原英文摘要：Person re-identification (ReID) is essential for applications such as access control and smart environments. While vision-based ReID has achieved impressive performance, it is vulnerable to illumination, clothing changes, and privacy concerns. Millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar offers a privacy-preserving and robust alternative, but practical radar-based ReID remains challenging due to sparse and noisy measurements as well as strong view-dependent distortions caused by radial sensing. In this paper, we propose mmReID, a radar-native gait-based ReID system that revisits feature representation and modeling from the perspective of mmWave sensing. Instead of converting radar point clouds into Cartesian coordinates, mmReID operates directly in the polar domain, preserving the inherent sensing structure and avoiding noise amplification induced by angular uncertainty. To address walking direction-dependent gait distortion, mmReID incorporates a radial perspective alignment strategy that maps observations from arbitrary walking directions into a predefined radial reference direction using a learned affine transformation operator, yielding consistent gait features. 中文摘要：嗓音障碍往往在语音质量明显下降之前，就以声带振动中的细微机械不规则性开始。对此类异常的早期识别很有价值，因为及时干预可能防止其进展到更晚期阶段。然而，在临床环境之外检测这些微弱的早期线索仍然具有挑战性：基于麦克风的分析所观测的是经声学塑形后的信号，该信号可能仅微弱地表现声源层面的不规则性，而基于接触的振动传感器则受低用户依从性困扰。我们提出 VibraGlot，一种非接触式筛查系统，它利用毫米波雷达在短时、非语言性的持续发声（3-5 s）期间感知颈部表面微振动。VibraGlot 没有采用通用的端到端学习，而是采用运动学优先的设计。它首先通过可观测性感知锁定来分离喉部区域，以抑制静态杂波，然后在规范相位域中执行周期同步重建。关键的是，该流程将违反模板的运动显式保留为结构化残差，把不规则的机械行为转化为诊断特征，而不是将其作为噪声平滑掉。我们在一个包含 74 名受试者、涵盖五种常见嗓音病理和健康对照的临床队列上评估 VibraGlot，该队列采集于医院和家庭环境。在严格的最小输入协议下，该系统取得超过 0.91 的 macro-F1 分数。这些结果表明，基于雷达的颈部表面运动学为可扩展的早期嗓音障碍筛查提供了一条稳健、可解释且非接触的途径，尤其是在病理仍表现为细微机械不规则性时。
