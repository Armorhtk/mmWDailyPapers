## 4D-RaDiff：面向4D雷达点云生成的潜在点扩散模型 [M]

原英文标题：4D-RaDiff: Latent Point Diffusion for 4D Radar Point Cloud Generation

作者：Jimmie Kwok, Holger Caesar, Andras Palffy

出版来源：ECCV 2026 Workshop DriveX Poster

出版时间：2026-08-16

原英文摘要：Automotive radar has shown promising developments in environment perception due to its cost-effectiveness and robustness in adverse weather conditions. However, the limited availability of annotated radar data poses a significant challenge for advancing radar-based perception systems. To address this limitation, we propose a novel framework to generate 4D radar point clouds for training and evaluating object detectors. Unlike image-based diffusion, our method is designed to consider the sparsity and unique characteristics of radar point clouds by applying diffusion to a latent point cloud representation. Within this latent space, generation is controlled via conditioning at either the object or scene level. The proposed 4D-RaDiff converts unlabeled bounding boxes into high-quality radar annotations and transforms existing LiDAR point cloud data into realistic radar scenes. Experiments demonstrate that incorporating synthetic radar data of 4D-RaDiff as data augmentation method during training of object detection models consistently improves performance compared to training on real data only. In addition, pre-training on our synthetic radar data achieves competitive detection performance, providing a promising, scalable approach to reduce dependence on costly manual annotations.

中文摘要：汽车雷达由于其在恶劣天气条件下的成本效益和鲁棒性，在环境感知方面显示出了有希望的发展。然而，带注释的雷达数据的有限可用性对推进基于雷达的感知系统提出了重大挑战。为了解决这一限制，我们提出了一种新颖的框架来生成 4D 雷达点云，用于训练和评估目标检测器。与基于图像的扩散不同，我们的方法旨在通过将扩散应用于潜在点云表示来考虑雷达点云的稀疏性和独特特征。在这个潜在空间中，生成是通过对象或场景级别的调节来控制的。所提出的 4D-RaDiff 将未标记的边界框转换为高质量的雷达注释，并将现有的 LiDAR 点云数据转换为真实的雷达场景。实验表明，与仅在真实数据上进行训练相比，在目标检测模型的训练过程中将 4D-RaDiff 的合成雷达数据合并为数据增强方法能够持续提高性能。此外，对我们的合成雷达数据进行预训练可实现具有竞争力的检测性能，提供一种有前途的、可扩展的方法来减少对昂贵的手动注释的依赖。

## CardiacMamba: 基于状态空间建模的公平鲁棒RGB-RF融合远程心率估计 [Preprint]

原英文标题：CardiacMamba: Fair and Robust RGB-RF Fusion for Remote Heart Rate Estimation via State Space Modeling

作者：Bo Zhao, Zheng Wu, Yiping Xie, Zitong YU

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-08-16

原英文摘要：Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) enables non-contact heart rate (HR) monitoring from facial videos, but RGB-only methods are vulnerable to illumination changes, motion artifacts, and skin-tone-dependent optical reflectance. We propose CardiacMamba, a fair and robust RGB-RF fusion framework that integrates optical facial cues and radio-frequency cardiac motion cues through state space modeling. CardiacMamba introduces a Temporal Difference Mamba Module (TDMM) to enhance subtle RF temporal variations, a bidirectional SSM-based interaction mechanism to align heterogeneous RGB-RF dynamics, and a Channel-wise Fast Fourier Transform (CFFT) module for channel-domain spectral refinement. On the EquiPleth dataset, CardiacMamba achieves state-of-the-art performance with 0.96 bpm MAE, 3.06 bpm RMSE, and 0.97 Pearson correlation, while reducing the observed light-dark skin-tone MAE gap to 0.26 bpm and maintaining robustness under RGB degradation and RF-missing conditions

## 基于辐射式无线本振分配的分布式FMCW雷达系统设计 [J]

原英文标题：Design of a Distributed FMCW Radar System Using Radiative Wireless LO Distribution

作者：Jae-Woo Park, Eunsung Jang, Kyunghwan Park, Jayol Lee, Seon-Ho Han, Jeong-Wook Kim

出版来源：IEEE Internet of Things Journal

出版时间：2026-08-13

原英文摘要：Conventional distributed Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave (FMCW) radar systems typically rely on wired reference distribution to maintain inter-node frequency and phase coherence, complicating field deployment and constraining flexible node placement. This paper presents a radiative wireless local-oscillator (LO) synchronization architecture that enables coherent multi-node FMCW operation without any physical reference link. A wide-beam stacked magnetic-dipole (MD) antenna is designed to radiate a stable LO signal from 4.15 to 4.40 GHz, providing synchronization coverage over a 90° azimuth sector to nodes within a few metres of the radiator. To mitigate LO-path-induced biases inherent to radiative distribution, a reference-node-based range-error calibration method and a distributed coherent combining algorithm are developed for accurate inter-node phase alignment. A hardware prototype is implemented in which the daughterboards receive the radiated LO and autonomously synthesize FMCW waveforms from 8.30 to 8.80 GHz. Outdoor experiments across multiple bistatic geometries confirm coherent operation and demonstrate consistent signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) improvements that average approximately 6.0 dB and reach up to 7.8 dB relative to the weaker individual node. A complementary indoor campaign in a multipath-rich environment reproduces the coherent gain in every trial, and extension to three cooperating nodes is verified in simulation. To the best of our knowledge, this work constitutes an early experimental proof-of-concept of radiative wireless LO synchronization as a deployment-friendly alternative to wired reference distribution for coherent distributed FMCW radar sensing.

中文摘要：传统的分布式调频连续波 (FMCW) 雷达系统通常依赖有线参考分布来维持节点间频率和相位相干性，从而使现场部署复杂化并限制灵活的节点放置。本文提出了一种辐射无线本地振荡器 (LO) 同步架构，无需任何物理参考链路即可实现相干多节点 FMCW 操作。宽波束堆叠磁偶极子 (MD) 天线设计用于辐射 4.15 至 4.40 GHz 的稳定 LO 信号，为辐射器几米范围内的节点提供 90° 方位扇区的同步覆盖。为了减轻本振路径引起的辐射分布固有的偏差，开发了一种基于参考节点的距离误差校准方法和分布式相干组合算法，以实现精确的节点间相位对准。实现了一个硬件原型，其中子板接收辐射 LO 并自动合成 8.30 至 8.80 GHz 的 FMCW 波形。跨多个双基地几何结构的户外实验证实了相干操作，并展示了一致的信噪比 (SNR) 改进，相对于较弱的单个节点，平均约为 6.0 dB，最高可达 7.8 dB。在多路径丰富的环境中进行互补的室内活动在每次试验中都重现了相干增益，并且在模拟中验证了扩展到三个协作节点的效果。据我们所知，这项工作构成了辐射无线 LO 同步的早期实验概念验证，作为相干分布式 FMCW 雷达传感的有线参考分布的部署友好替代方案。

## DISADet：用于4D雷达-相机三维目标检测的深度交互与语义对齐 [M]

原英文标题：DISADet: Depth interaction and semantic alignment for 4D radar-camera 3D object detection

作者：Hang Yan, Peifeng Cheng, Xiaokai Bai, Luping Wang, Wuming Wu

出版来源：Displays

出版时间：2026-08-01

原英文摘要：4D millimeter-wave (MMW) radar, with its high resolution, low cost, and reliable performance in adverse conditions, is gaining attention for 3D object detection. Nevertheless, existing fusion frameworks still face two major challenges: cross-modal depth misalignment and heterogeneous bird’s-eye-view (BEV) feature integration. To address these issues, we propose DISADet, a radar–camera fusion framework based on depth interaction and semantic alignment. Specifically, we present a novel cross-modal gated Mamba (CMGM) module that complements local attention by modeling long-range depth dependencies, thereby achieving more accurate depth prediction. We then use the fused depth to guide bidirectional projection and perform forward–backward BEV fusion, enhancing detection performance in both near and far ranges. Finally, we propose a cross-modal semantic alignment (CMSA) module to reduce the gap between heterogeneous modalities. This module includes cross-attention and semantic consistency modules, the former used to model interactions between modalities and the latter to reinforce alignment via cosine similarity. Extensive experiments on public datasets show that DISADet outperforms state-of-the-art approaches in accuracy, improving 3D mAP by 1.72% on TJ4DRadSet and driving corridor (DC) mAP by 1.58% on VoD.

中文摘要：4D毫米波（MMW）雷达凭借其高分辨率、低成本和在恶劣条件下可靠的性能，在3D物体检测中越来越受到关注。尽管如此，现有的融合框架仍然面临两大挑战：跨模态深度错位和异构鸟瞰（BEV）特征集成。为了解决这些问题，我们提出了 DISADet，一种基于深度交互和语义对齐的雷达相机融合框架。具体来说，我们提出了一种新颖的跨模式门控 Mamba (CMGM) 模块，该模块通过建模远程深度依赖性来补充局部注意力，从而实现更准确的深度预测。然后，我们使用融合的深度来引导双向投影并执行前向-后向 BEV 融合，从而增强近距离和远距离的检测性能。最后，我们提出了跨模态语义对齐（CMSA）模块来减少异构模态之间的差距。该模块包括交叉注意和语义一致性模块，前者用于对模态之间的交互进行建模，后者用于通过余弦相似度来加强对齐。对公共数据集的大量实验表明，DISADet 在准确性方面优于最先进的方法，在 TJ4DRadSet 上将 3D mAP 提高了 1.72%，在 VoD 上将驾驶走廊 (DC) mAP 提高了 1.58%。

## 基于雷达网络的分布式目标跟踪 [Preprint]

原英文标题：Distributed Target Tracking using Radar Networks

作者：Shao-Hsuan Hung, Raj Thilak Rajan

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-08-19

原英文摘要：Distributed target tracking is essential for scalable and robust sensing systems, as it enables multiple radar nodes to cooperatively estimate a target state without relying on a centralized fusion center. In this paper, we present a fully distributed framework for single-target tracking in frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar networks. Each monostatic radar node observes local range and Doppler measurements and exchanges information only with neighboring nodes. Two consensus optimization-based estimators are developed. First, a Distributed Maximum A Posteriori (D-MAP) estimator is formulated for batch-based tracking, where prior state information is incorporated and the resulting optimization problem is solved using consensus-based alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM). Second, a Distributed Extended Kalman Filter (D-EKF) is proposed for recursive tracking, where each node performs local prediction and correction followed by consensus ADMM-based information exchange. We derive the posterior Cramér-Rao lower bound (PCRLB) as a theoretical performance benchmark. Our simulation results show that D-MAP improves the accuracy of the estimation over the distributed maximum-likelihood baseline. These results demonstrate that the proposed framework provides a scalable and robust alternative to centralized radar tracking, given only local inter-node communication.

中文摘要：分布式目标跟踪对于可扩展且稳健的传感系统至关重要，因为它使多个雷达节点能够协作估计目标状态，而无需依赖集中式融合中心。在本文中，我们提出了一个用于调频连续波（FMCW）雷达网络中单目标跟踪的完全分布式框架。每个单站雷达节点观察本地距离和多普勒测量结果，并仅与相邻节点交换信息。开发了两种基于共识优化的估计器。首先，为基于批量的跟踪制定了分布式最大后验概率 (D-MAP) 估计器，其中合并了先验状态信息，并使用基于共识的乘法器交替方向法 (ADMM) 解决了产生的优化问题。其次，提出了分布式扩展卡尔曼滤波器（D-EKF）用于递归跟踪，其中每个节点执行本地预测和校正，然后进行基于 ADMM 的一致信息交换。我们推导出后 Cramér-Rao 下界 (PCRLB) 作为理论性能基准。我们的模拟结果表明，D-MAP 提高了分布式最大似然基线估计的准确性。这些结果表明，在仅考虑本地节点间通信的情况下，所提出的框架为集中式雷达跟踪提供了可扩展且稳健的替代方案。

## DroneRFc-MM：反无人机多模态检测实测数据集 [M]

出版来源：电子与信息学报

出版时间：2025-03-10

原英文摘要：Multimodal data fusion can effectively improve the generalization, robustness, and scene adaptability of counter-unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) detection systems. To address the limitations of existing counter-UAV datasets in sensing modalities, UAV models, and annotation granularity, the paper releases DroneRFc-MM, a multimodal counter-UAV detection dataset. DroneRFc-MM synchronously collects data from six types of sensors, including a pan-tilt-zoom camera, a wide-angle (fisheye) camera, radio-frequency antennas, LiDAR, millimeter-wave radar, and a microphone array. The dataset covers six consumer UAV models and provides fine-grained annotations such as model category, position, attitude, and velocity. It supports multiple tasks, including target detection, UAV model recognition, and motion-direction reasoning, and is accompanied by user-friendly sample extraction tools. Finally, as a demonstration of dataset usage, the paper evaluates the performance of recent Qwen-series models on UAV flight-direction reasoning. Objective: The work aims to build a more comprehensive benchmark for multimodal counter-UAV detection. Existing datasets often cover limited sensing modalities and UAV models, making it difficult to represent realistic low-altitude scenarios and diverse target characteristics. Their annotations are also typically coarse, such as category labels or bounding boxes, and thus cannot fully support downstream tasks requiring precise spatial, motion, and cross-modal information. DroneRFc-MM addresses these gaps by providing synchronized multimodal data, richer UAV coverage, and fine-grained annotations for detection, model recognition, trajectory analysis, motion reasoning, and multimodal fusion evaluation. Methods: The DroneRFc-MM dataset was synchronously captured via six heterogeneous sensors—including a pan-tilt-zoom(PTZ) camera, fisheye camera, radio frequency(RF) antenna, LiDAR, millimeter-wave radar, and microphone array—on an open rooftop of a university in Zhejiang Province. All devices were time-synchronized via network timestamp, and drones flew in rectangular and vertical reciprocating trajectories within 20–60 meters. Fine-grained annotations including drone type, position, attitude and velocity were provided. For flight direction reasoning task, 5-second multimodal clips were generated: videos for cameras and RF spectrograms, audio for microphones, text coordinates for radar point clouds. Zero-shot inference was conducted on Qwen 3.6-Plus and Qwen 3.5-Omni-Plus models with unified prompts, and accuracy and inference time were evaluated by comparing predicted directions with ground truth calculated from drone positioning data. Conclusions: Experiments on Qwen-series models show that general-purpose multimodal large models can capture weak motion-related features from drone-related videos, audio, RF spectrograms and point clouds, but only achieve limited flight-direction reasoning accuracy ranging from 20% to 30%. Meanwhile, the long inference time and unstable latency make them unable to satisfy the real-time and stability demands of practical low-altitude surveillance systems. These results demonstrate that domain-specific pre-training, supervised fine-tuning, knowledge enhancement and lightweight inference optimization are essential for deploying multi-modal LLMs in real anti-UAV detection scenarios. Future work will focus on expanding the dataset scale and enriching application scenarios to support the development of intelligent and efficient low-altitude airspace management systems.

## DRONet：稀疏域表示中原始雷达数据的精细检测 [J]

原英文标题：DRONet: Fine Detection From Raw Radar Data in Sparse Domain Representations

作者：Sheng Wang, Qiao Wang, Xujie Gao, Kexin Chen, Linzhe Yang, Boxun Han, Guodong Sun, Fu Xu

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems

出版时间：2026-08-11

原英文摘要：Radar sensing is essential to autonomous driving and intelligent transportation systems, as it remains reliable under adverse weather and illumination conditions. However, conventional radar signal processing pipelines are limited by the non-stationary characteristics of radar echoes and the time-varying properties of their spectra. Fixed-band models cannot adapt to these variations, and the lack of end-to-end optimization between sampling and detection introduces feature misalignment and information loss. This paper proposes the Dynamic Radar Optimization Network (DRONet), which addresses these limitations through three components: 1) a frequency-decoupled attention mechanism that re-weights Doppler sub-bands according to an information entropy criterion, relaxing the constraints of static frequency modeling; 2) a learnable sparse sampling module that optimizes the trade-off between information preservation and reconstruction accuracy; and 3) an end-to-end joint optimization scheme that couples the sampling and detection tasks within a single training framework, mitigating the feature drift of cascaded architectures. On the RADIal dataset, DRONet attains the highest average recall (91.38%) and the lowest computational cost (496 GFLOPs) among the compared methods, with competitive detection precision and an inference latency of 23.6 ms per frame.

中文摘要：雷达传感对于自动驾驶和智能交通系统至关重要，因为它在恶劣的天气和照明条件下仍然可靠。然而，传统的雷达信号处理流程受到雷达回波的非平稳特性及其频谱的时变特性的限制。固定频带模型无法适应这些变化，并且采样和检测之间缺乏端到端优化会导致特征错位和信息丢失。本文提出了动态雷达优化网络（DRONet），它通过三个组成部分解决了这些限制：1）频率解耦注意机制，根据信息熵准则对多普勒子带重新加权，放松静态频率建模的约束； 2）可学习的稀疏采样模块，优化信息保存和重建精度之间的权衡； 3）端到端联合优化方案，将采样和检测任务耦合在单个训练框架内，减轻级联架构的特征漂移。在 RADIal 数据集上，DRONet 在比较方法中获得了最高的平均召回率 (91.38%) 和最低的计算成本 (496 GFLOPs)，具有竞争性的检测精度和每帧 23.6 毫秒的推理延迟。
