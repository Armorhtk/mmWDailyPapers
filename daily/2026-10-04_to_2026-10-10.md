## 用于卒中患者步态评估的非接触式 AI 辅助传感器

原英文标题：A contactless and AI-assisted sensor for gait assessment of stroke patients

作者：Zhuangzhuang Gu, Ryan Titus, Hem Regmi, Souvik Sen, Sanjib Sur

出版来源：Computers in Biology and Medicine

出版时间：2026-10-01

原英文摘要：The gait parameters, i.e., gait speed, stride length, step length, and cadence, serve as key indicators for patients with mobility impairments. Monitoring these parameters can significantly aid in assessing recovery progress, especially in stroke patients. Compared to traditional clinical visits, wearable sensors, or motion capture systems, millimeter-wave (mmWave) device offers unique advantages: low-cost, contactless, light-free, and no privacy invasion, making frequent gait monitoring feasible for home deployment. We introduce mmGait, an mmWave-based system that extracts foot features from reflections and learns walking patterns to accurately predict the 3D locations of both feet, from which we can derive the gait parameters automatically without requiring subjects to wear any sensors or be recorded by cameras. Across multiple stroke patients, mmGait achieves low median errors in estimating key gait parameters: less than 0.03 m/s for gait speed, ∼3 cm for stride length, 7.4 cm for step length, and below 10 steps/min for cadence. Agreement analysis further demonstrates good agreement with the OptiTrack reference for gait speed and stride length, supporting the feasibility of the proposed contactless approach for coarse spatiotemporal gait assessment.

中文摘要：步态参数，即步速、步幅、步长和步频，是行动障碍患者的关键指标。监测这些参数可以显著帮助评估恢复进展，尤其是对卒中患者。与传统临床就诊、可穿戴传感器或运动捕捉系统相比，毫米波（mmWave）设备具有独特优势：低成本、非接触、无需光照、不侵犯隐私，使得频繁步态监测在家庭部署中可行。我们提出 mmGait，一个基于 mmWave 的系统，它从反射中提取足部特征并学习行走模式，以准确预测双脚的 3D 位置，从中我们可以自动推导步态参数，而无需受试者佩戴任何传感器或被摄像头记录。在多名卒中患者中，mmGait 在估计关键步态参数时实现了较低的中位误差：步速小于 0.03 m/s，步幅约 3 cm，步长 7.4 cm，步频低于 10 步/分。一致性分析进一步表明，在步速和步幅方面与 OptiTrack 参考具有良好一致性，支持所提出的非接触式方法用于粗略时空步态评估的可行性。

## 基于 MIMO 雷达的穿墙人体识别位置不变框架

原英文标题：A Position-Invariant Framework for Through-Wall Human Identification Using MIMO Radar

作者：Changlong Wang, Chao Xu, Hengyi Ren, Chong Han, Lijuan Sun

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing

出版时间：2026-10-02

原英文摘要：Through-wall human identification using multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) radar is a challenging task due to severe pose variations, dynamic spatial positions, and the strong coupling between identity-related features and absolute target locations. Existing approaches often suffer from degraded generalization when subjects move freely or operate at varying distances, which limits their applicability in real-world scenarios. In this paper, we propose PI-TWHID, a position-invariant framework for through-wall human identification using MIMO radar. The core idea is to explicitly decouple identity representation from spatial position by jointly leveraging a position-aware sample pairing strategy and a hierarchical pose-prior-guided identity learning network. During training, positive and negative sample pairs are constructed across different spatial locations to enforce cross-position consistency in the feature space, while a hierarchical pose-guided fusion mechanism continuously injects human structural priors into radar feature learning, effectively suppressing pose- and position-induced feature distortions. Notably, the proposed framework adopts a fully radar-based inference pipeline. Human pose cues are estimated directly from radar signals during deployment, while optical motion capture is used only for training supervision, enabling practical and standalone radar-only deployment without any external sensing infrastructure. Extensive experiments conducted under free-movement conditions demonstrate that the proposed method achieves an identification accuracy of 97.85% through a 24 cm brick wall and 98.39% in line-of-sight scenarios, consistently outperforming representative state-of-the-art methods with an average inference time of 0.05 s per frame.

中文摘要：使用多输入多输出（MIMO）雷达进行穿墙人体识别是一项具有挑战性的任务，原因在于严重的姿态变化、动态空间位置，以及身份相关特征与目标绝对位置之间的强耦合。现有方法在受试者自由移动或在不同距离下活动时，往往会出现泛化性能下降，这限制了它们在实际场景中的适用性。本文提出 PI-TWHID，一种使用 MIMO 雷达进行穿墙人体识别的位置不变框架。其核心思想是通过联合利用位置感知样本配对策略和分层姿态先验引导的身份学习网络，显式地将身份表征与空间位置解耦。训练期间，在不同空间位置构建正样本对和负样本对，以在特征空间中强制跨位置一致性；同时，分层姿态引导融合机制持续将人体结构先验注入雷达特征学习，有效抑制由姿态和位置引起的特征失真。值得注意的是，所提框架采用完全基于雷达的推理流程。在部署期间，人体姿态线索直接从雷达信号中估计，而光学动作捕捉仅用于训练监督，从而实现无需任何外部传感基础设施的实用且独立的仅雷达部署。在自由移动条件下进行的大量实验表明，所提方法在穿过 24 cm 砖墙时达到 97.85% 的识别准确率，在视距场景中达到 98.39%，并以每帧 0.05 s 的平均推理时间持续优于代表性最先进方法。

## 鲁棒学习框架：部分误标注训练数据下基于深度学习的雷达目标检测

原英文标题：A Robust Learning Framework for Deep-Learning-Based Radar Target Detection With Partially Mislabeled Training Data

作者：Chuanfei Zang, Yiru Lin, Yumiao Wang, Xingyu Chen, Xiaobo Yang, Guolong Cui

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-10-05

原英文摘要：Deep-learning-based radar target detection has substantially improved detection performance, but its effectiveness depends critically on the correctness of the training labels assigned to radar echo samples. In practical applications, training data may contain partially mislabeled samples, which can cause detection models to learn biased supervisory information and thereby degrade detection performance. To address this issue, this paper proposes SL-RLF, a robust learning framework for deep-learning-based radar target detection with partially mislabeled training data. Specifically, we establish a probabilistic model to characterize the generation mechanisms of label errors in radar target detection and analyze the robustness of detection models under partial label errors within a risk minimization formulation. Theoretical analysis shows that, under appropriate assumptions, a loss function satisfying the symmetry condition enables the detection model in the learning framework to be robust against partial label errors. Guided by this result, we design a loss function satisfying the symmetry condition and construct the proposed robust learning framework, SL-RLF. Theoretically, under the corresponding label-error conditions, the optimal detection model learned by SL-RLF from partially mislabeled training data can achieve the same detection performance under the true data distribution as the optimal model learned from accurately labeled data. Experimental results demonstrate that, under different types and levels of partial label errors, the proposed SL-RLF consistently outperforms representative methods, including Co-teaching and TCE, and its robustness is generally consistent with the theoretical analysis.

中文摘要：基于深度学习的雷达目标检测已显著提升检测性能，但其有效性关键取决于分配给雷达回波样本的训练标签是否正确。在实际应用中，训练数据可能包含部分误标样本，这会导致检测模型学习到有偏的监督信息，从而降低检测性能。为解决这一问题，本文提出 SL-RLF，一种用于具有部分误标训练数据的基于深度学习的雷达目标检测的鲁棒学习框架。具体而言，我们建立一个概率模型来刻画雷达目标检测中标签错误的生成机制，并在风险最小化形式下分析部分标签错误下检测模型的鲁棒性。理论分析表明，在适当假设下，满足对称条件的损失函数能使学习框架中的检测模型对部分标签错误具有鲁棒性。在该结果的指导下，我们设计了一个满足对称条件的损失函数，并构建了所提出的鲁棒学习框架 SL-RLF。理论上，在相应的标签错误条件下，SL-RLF 从部分误标训练数据中学习到的最优检测模型在真实数据分布下能够达到与从准确标注数据中学习到的最优模型相同的检测性能。实验结果表明，在不同类型和程度的部分标签错误下，所提出的 SL-RLF 始终优于代表性方法，包括 Co-teaching 和 TCE，且其鲁棒性总体与理论分析一致。

## 基于 LLM 的多模态框架：面向全面的 mmWave 数据合成、理解与推理

原英文标题：An LLM-based Multi-modal Framework for Comprehensive mmWave Data Synthesis, Understanding, and Reasoning

作者：Yifan Yan, Xiuzhen Guo, Zikang Zhang, Yaobin Zhu, Xiangguang Wang, Yuanchao Shu, Jiming Chen

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing

出版时间：2026-10-01

原英文摘要：Millimeter-wave (mmWave) sensing is promising for human-centric applications, but most existing systems remain limited to closed-set recognition and cannot directly describe observed motion or answer follow-up questions. A key bottleneck is the lack of scalable radar-language supervision: real mmWave data is costly to collect and difficult to annotate with rich semantic descriptions. We present mmExpert, a data-and-model framework for language-grounded mmWave understanding. mmExpert couples a text-to-mmWave data flywheel, which converts textual priors into curated radar-language supervision through motion generation, electromagnetic simulation, domain randomization, and iterative curation, with WaveLLM, a mmWave-to-text model that generates grounded captions and targeted QA responses from micro-Doppler inputs. We evaluate the framework with complementary synthetic-to-real diagnostics, controlled baseline comparisons, captioning and QA benchmarks, target-domain analysis, and real-world indoor tests. Across virtual and real mmWave settings, WaveLLM achieves the strongest overall mmWave-to-text performance among the compared methods, while target-domain results show the benefit of target-conditioned synthetic supervision. These results indicate that scalable radarlanguage synthesis can support mmWave-to-text learning, with synthetic data as the main supervision source and limited real data used for calibration and held-out evaluation.

中文摘要：毫米波（mmWave）感知在以人为中心的应用中具有前景，但大多数现有系统仍局限于闭集识别，无法直接描述观察到的运动或回答后续问题。一个关键瓶颈是缺乏可扩展的雷达语言监督：真实毫米波数据采集成本高，且难以用丰富的语义描述进行标注。我们提出 mmExpert，一个用于语言接地的毫米波理解的数据与模型框架。mmExpert 将文本到毫米波数据飞轮与 WaveLLM 耦合：前者通过运动生成、电磁仿真、域随机化和迭代策展，将文本先验转化为经过策展的雷达语言监督；后者是一个毫米波到文本模型，从微多普勒输入生成有依据的描述和针对性问答回复。我们用互补的合成到真实诊断、受控基线比较、描述生成和问答基准、目标域分析以及真实世界室内测试来评估该框架。在虚拟和真实毫米波设置中，WaveLLM 在比较方法中取得最强的整体毫米波到文本性能，而目标域结果表明了目标条件合成监督的收益。这些结果表明，可扩展的雷达语言合成可以支持毫米波到文本学习，其中合成数据作为主要监督来源，有限真实数据用于校准和留出评估。

## 使用 mmWave 穿烟稠密 3D 感知

原英文标题：Dense 3D Perception Through Smoke Using mmWave

作者：Bin Zhao, Patrick Chiou, Teerth Patel, Nakul Garg

出版来源：Proceedings of the 17th ACM Workshop on Wireless of the Students, by the Students, for the Students

出版时间：2026-10-26

原英文摘要：Dense 3D depth sensing that relies on optical sensors becomes unreliable when smoke or fog obscures the scene. Under such visual degradation, mmWave radar operates reliably, but its limited angular resolution produces depth estimates that preserve metric scale but have incomplete spatial structure. We present GRADE, a framework for generative radar depth estimation under visual degradation without a synthetic aperture. GRADE first transforms raw 4D radar spectra into coarse metric depth. A radar-conditioned diffusion model, initialized with pretrained geometric priors, then recovers finer spatial structure. In parallel, a ControlNet-style RGB adapter branch provides image-aligned corrections when useful visual evidence remains. We train and evaluate GRADE on approximately 95K synchronized frames collected across 12 buildings on our campus, more than 40K of which were captured in real smoke. On test sequences from buildings excluded from training, GRADE outperforms representative baseline methods in both clear and smoke scenarios, demonstrating that generative refinement can improve limited-aperture radar depth while maintaining reliability as visibility decreases.

## 基于 YOLOv8 与毫米波雷达融合的新能源智能网联汽车行人检测算法设计

原英文标题：Design of pedestrian detection algorithm for new energy intelligent connected vehicles based on the fusion of YOLOv8 and millimeter-wave radar

作者：ShengWang Xia

出版来源：Discover Applied Sciences

出版时间：2026-10-01

原英文摘要：Pedestrian detection for new energy intelligent connected vehicles faces dual challenges of accuracy and real‑time performance, while single sensors exhibit poor adaptability to complex scenarios including nighttime, rain, fog and occlusion. This paper proposes a pedestrian detection algorithm based on deep fusion of YOLOv8 and millimeter‑wave radar. First, for the visual branch, we reconstruct the C2f module with lightweight channel‑space joint attention and introduce a high‑resolution P2 detection head to strengthen small‑target feature representation. Second, a cross‑modal deformable attention (CMDA) module is developed to compensate geometric mismatches between sparse radar point clouds and visual features via learnable spatial offsets. Different from conventional deformable attention, CMDA adopts heterogeneous cross‑modal query‑value asymmetry, physics‑constrained offset generation derived from radar range‑velocity priors, and sigmoid‑gated adaptive modal weighting. SIoU loss and augmented complex‑scene samples are utilized in training to enhance model generalization. Evaluated on nuScenes, VoD and self‑built datasets, the proposed algorithm achieves 79.4% pedestrian AP and obtains 76.9% AP under nighttime conditions. With 14.3 M parameters, it runs at 38.6 FPS on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin and satisfies in‑vehicle real‑time requirements. This work provides an engineering‑feasible solution for pedestrian safety perception and offers methodological references for multimodal deep fusion paradigms.

## Dirichlet Splatting：面向基于波的反问题的可微渲染

原英文标题：Dirichlet Splatting: Differentiable Rendering for Wave-Based Inverse Problems

作者：Xingyu Chen, Wuqiong Zhao, Xinyu Zhang, Tzu-Mao Li

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-09-30

原英文摘要：Wave-based coherent imaging, including terahertz tomography, synthetic-aperture acoustics, and millimeter-wave radar, forms images by Fourier-processing finite-length signals, with an exact point spread function that is not Gaussian but a Dirichlet kernel: complex-valued, oscillatory, and periodic. However, transplanting 3D Gaussian splatting to coherent sensing fails by construction; Gaussian splats discard the sidelobe energy (10-20% of the total) and the phase that governs coherent interference between reflectors. Our key idea is to replace the learned Gaussian footprint with the physically exact Dirichlet kernel of the finite-window DFT, modulated by a surfel that carries area, normal, and material, so that the rendering primitive matches the measurement physics instead of approximating it. We pair this primitive with a specialized solver, Dirichlet Sliding Frank-Wolfe (DSFW), that combines variable projection, residual dual certificates, and certificate-driven hard replacement of low-utility surfels, with periodic low-resolution coupled Levenberg-Marquardt correction, navigating the rugged loss landscape that breaks generic first-order optimizers.
