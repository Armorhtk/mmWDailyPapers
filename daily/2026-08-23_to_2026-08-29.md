## 基于FMCW雷达和自适应VMD辅助深度学习的非接触式ECG重建方法

原英文标题：A Non-Contact ECG Reconstruction Method Based on FMCW Radar and Adaptive VMD-Assisted Deep Learning

作者：Xiaolong Chen, Yonggang Luo, Long Zhang, Yinwei Li, Yiming Zhu

出版来源：IEEE Open Journal of Signal Processing

出版时间：2026-08-24

原英文摘要：To address the limitations of conventional ECG monitoring, including the need for direct skin contact, discomfort during long-term use, and limited applicability in special monitoring scenarios, this paper proposes a non-contact ECG reconstruction method based on FMCW radar and deep learning. In the signal preprocessing stage, adaptive Variational Mode Decomposition (VMD) optimized by Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) is introduced to suppress respiratory harmonic interference and improve heartbeat signal separation. In the reconstruction stage, a deep learning framework integrating convolutional neural networks, multi-head attention, and LSTM is developed to capture local temporal features and global sequential dependencies, enabling the mapping from radar-derived heartbeat signals to ECG waveforms. Experiments on a public dataset and additional hospital collected data demonstrate that the proposed method can reconstruct ECG signals with high waveform consistency relative to reference ECGs. Quantitative results show that the reconstructed signals achieve low RMSE, high PCC, and good RR-interval agreement with the ground-truth ECG. The results indicate the feasibility of the proposed method for non-contact ECG reconstruction and suggest its potential for continuous physiological monitoring.

中文摘要：针对传统ECG监测需要直接接触皮肤、长期使用不适以及在特殊监测场景下适用性有限等局限，本文提出了一种基于FMCW雷达和深度学习的非接触式ECG重建方法。在信号预处理阶段，引入由蚁群算法(ACO)优化的自适应变分模态分解(VMD)，以抑制呼吸谐波干扰并改善心跳信号分离。在重建阶段，开发了一种融合卷积神经网络、多头注意力和LSTM的深度学习框架，用于捕捉局部时间特征和全局序列依赖关系，从而实现从雷达衍生的心跳信号到ECG波形的映射。在公共数据集和额外医院采集数据上的实验表明，所提方法能够重建出与参考ECG波形一致性较高的ECG信号。定量结果显示，重建信号相对于真实ECG具有较低的RMSE、较高的PCC以及良好的RR间期一致性。结果表明，所提方法用于非接触式ECG重建具有可行性，并显示出其在连续生理监测中的应用潜力。

## 用于毫米波手势识别的新型组合双网络

原英文标题：A Novel Combined Dual Network for mmWave Hand Gesture Recognition

作者：Xiangqun Zhang, Zhizhou Ge, Meiqi Zhou, Genyuan Du, Guolong Cui, Jie Liu

出版来源：Radio Science

出版时间：2026-08-25

原英文摘要：Abstract Achieving an optimal balance between recognition accuracy and computational efficiency remains a pivotal challenge in millimeter‐wave radar‐based hand gesture recognition systems. In this paper, we propose a novel method that integrates a Dual‐stream RepViT and Multi‐teacher Knowledge Transfer Network (DRMKT‐Net). The proposed dual‐stream RepViT backbone effectively extracts spatiotemporal features from both Range‐Time Maps and Doppler‐Time Maps. On our custom data set with 22 gesture classes, the D‐RepViT model achieves state‐of‐the‐art recognition accuracy of 99.33%. Then, the model parameters are compressed, and, through a collaborative multi‐teacher knowledge‐transfer mechanism, the student model is reduced to only 27% of its original parameter count. Comparative experiments demonstrate that our approach significantly outperforms existing methods in terms of both recognition performance and computational efficiency. The proposed method exhibits promising potential for deployment in resource‐constrained embedded devices for real‐time human–computer interaction applications.

## 用于驾驶员疲劳检测的基于场景的持续学习框架，采用三维空间-多普勒融合

原英文标题：A Scenario-Based Continual Learning Framework for Driver Fatigue Detection Using 3D Spatial–Doppler Fusion

作者：Yao Ge, Shibo Li, Yuqing Zhu, Mengxuan Sun, Zhengjia Xu, Chengkai Tang, Qammer H. Abbasi, Chong Tang

出版来源：IEEE Internet of Things Journal

出版时间：2026-08-21

原英文摘要：Driver fatigue detection is crucial for road safety, and mmWave radar provides a privacy-preserving approach for monitoring fatigue-related driver behaviours. Radar-based driver fatigue detection must remain reliable across changing in-cabin sensing scenarios, including variations in radar–driver geometry, sensor placement, and surrounding reflections. Existing models often degrade under unseen radar sensing conditions, while sequential adaptation to these conditions can cause catastrophic forgetting of previously learned scenarios. To address this, we propose a scenario-based continual-learning framework that uniquely fuses 3D spatial radar cubes with micro-Doppler maps for a driver state representation. The core of our framework is a novel continual-learning strategy named Elastic Prompt-Backbone (EPB), which synergizes a spatial enhanced transformer architecture with tailored regularization and prompt-based learning mechanisms. EPB effectively mitigates catastrophic forgetting by introducing Hierarchical Elastic Consolidation (HEC) to preserve foundational knowledge at different network layers, and Prompt-Conditioned Fisher (PCF) to dynamically modulate learning based on task relevance. This dual approach enables the model to continuously adapt to new driving scenarios while maintaining high performance on previously learned tasks. With the 10% replay-buffer setting used in the main comparison, EPB achieves an average incremental accuracy of 84.98 ± 0.50% while limiting Forgetting to 3.99 ± 0.26%. Moreover, with only a 1% replay buffer, EPB still outperforms strong replay-based baselines using a 10% buffer. To the best of our knowledge, this is among the first works to formulate radar-based driver fatigue detection as a scenario-incremental continual learning problem. Our code has been published in https://github.com/BarryTang22/radar-cl-epb.git.

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing

原英文摘要：A common challenge in mmWave sensing applications is generalization. The performance of machine learning methods drastically degrades when faced with configuration changes, primarily due to the limited training data available. Unlike factors such as unseen users, user environments, and test categories, user positions and orientations are continuously variable and numerous. Collecting data for all potential locations and orientations requires an impractical amount of training data at a high human cost, which is unacceptable in real-world deployments. In this paper, we propose GR-LO, a mmWave-based gesture recognition system that reliably identifies user gestures, irrespective of their spatial position or orientation. The key insight of GR-LO is synthesizing mmWave sensing data to enhance the generalization ability of mmWave-based sensing systems. GR-LO addresses this challenge by constructing a physics-based transformation vector and employing a controlled diffusion generative framework to synthesize and expand mmWave sensing data. The model is trained only once and then calibrated with a few real samples at inference, enabling generalization across arbitrary execution locations and orientations within the radar sensing area. To extract consistent features from diverse patterns within the same category while minimizing computational cost, we design M-Net, a lightweight, high-precision network for low-latency gesture recognition. Our extensive real-world experiments demonstrate GR-LO's effectiveness in mmWave gesture recognition, enhancing generalization across different user locations and orientations without the need for laborious data collection.

原英文摘要：Over the past decade, the fusion of light detection and ranging (LiDAR) and inertial navigation systems (INS) has become a reliable solution for environmental perception in intelligent mobile platforms. However, LiDAR performance degrades under adverse weather conditions. Millimeter-wave radar offers complementary benefits: it is robust to environmental disturbances and provides direct Doppler velocity measurements. Moreover, most existing fusion frameworks rely on the extended Kalman filter (EKF), which suffers from linearization errors and inconsistency. To address these limitations, we propose and derive a tightly-coupled LiDAR–radar–inertial odometry framework based on the equivariant filter (EqF). The framework leverages both LiDAR and radar to provide complementary constraints on the 9-dimensional navigation state, significantly improving accuracy and robustness in challenging environments. Furthermore, we conduct an in-depth observability analysis using Lie derivative theory, examining both the nonlinear system and its discrete filter system. We show that the EqF preserves the same unobservable directions as the underlying nonlinear system, thereby ensuring consistent state estimation, a property that standard EKF does not possess. In addition, experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate that our method outperforms the state-of-the-art LiDAR–inertial odometry approach and other EKF-based approaches in terms of localization accuracy, robustness and velocity estimation precision. Note to Practitioners—This work aims to provide a high-precision and robust fusion framework for LiDAR, radar, and INS in intelligent mobile platforms such as autonomous vehicles and drones. On such platforms, most existing EKF-based LiDAR-inertial odometry methods suffer from performance degradation under adverse weather conditions and from the inherent inconsistency of the EKF. To address these challenges, we propose a tightly-coupled LiDAR–radar–inertial odometry framework based on the EqF. The approach exploits the symmetry of the semi-direct product group to jointly model the navigation state and IMU biases within a geometrically consistent structure. We further conduct an in-depth observability analysis, demonstrating that the proposed framework preserves the correct unobservable directions of the underlying nonlinear system. As a result, the system exhibits superior robustness against initialization errors and external disturbances, along with improved accuracy compared to traditional approaches.

## 基于高斯混合模型的FMCW雷达点云数据引导用于PointNet跌倒检测

原英文标题：Bootstrapping Point Cloud Data from FMCW Radar Using Gaussian Mixture Model for PointNet-based Fall Detection

作者：Muhamad A. F. Z. Akbar, Fiky Y. Suratman, Abdelhak M. Zoubir

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Journal

出版时间：2026-08-24

原英文摘要：FMCW radar is increasingly adopted as a privacy-preserving solution for Human Activity Recognition. However, the point cloud data generated by this radar often exhibits inconsistent point counts across frames due to variations in signal reflectivity, multipath effects, and target motion dynamics. This instability presents a significant challenge for point cloud–based deep learning models such as PointNet and PointNet++, which require fixed-size inputs to ensure stable feature extraction. To address this issue, this study proposes a parametric boot-strapping method based on a Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM) to normalize the number of points in each data window while preserving the original spatial structure of the radar measurements. In this approach, the GMM is trained using three-dimensional spatial features (x, y, z), followed by a resampling process to produce a uniform number of points. Subsequently, the Doppler velocity (v d )–which cannot be directly modeled by the GMM—is restored using a nearest-neighbor assignment strategy in the spatial domain to maintain consistency between spatial and motion information. The pre-processing pipeline also includes outlier removal using DBSCAN and temporal segmentation based on windowing. The proposed method is evaluated using two deep learning architectures (PointNet and PointNet++) for the fall detection task. Experimental results show substantial performance improvements, with PointNet++ achieving an accuracy of up to 99.8%. These findings highlight the importance of point-count normalization using the bootstrap and the integration of Doppler information for enhancing the reliability of radar-based activity recognition systems. This method provides a strong foundation for future research, including extensions to a wider range of activity classes, multi-object scenarios, and outdoor environments.
