## 边缘端使用机器学习的4D雷达数据处理 [J]

原英文标题：4D Radar Data Processing at the Edge using Machine Learning

作者：Adrien Hernandez, Alain Pegatoquet, Jean-Yves Dauvignac, H. Passet

出版来源：IEEE Sensors Journal

出版时间：2026-08-01

原英文摘要：Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) rely heavily on robust radar sensing to ensure safety across diverse environmental conditions. Deep Learning has revolutionized radar signal processing, offering superior detection capabilities over classical CFAR methods. However, the memory footprint and the high computational cost of these models pose a significant barrier to their deployment on resource-constrained automotive edge devices. This paper presents a comprehensive study on the feasibility and portability of a radar tensor processing network. We conducted a rigorous analysis of various backbone ML architectures and performed code profiling to identify processing bottlenecks. Furthermore, we benchmarked the model on the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin platform. By implementing a customized optimization pipeline—including INT8 quantization, TensorRT compilation, and input subsampling—we successfully reduced inference latency by a factor of 6, achieving a frame rate of 11.2 FPS (89ms). These results quantitatively demonstrate that with hardware-aware optimization, advanced deep learning based radar perception is viable for real-time ADAS applications.

中文摘要：先进驾驶辅助系统（ADAS）在很大程度上依赖稳健的雷达感知，以确保在不同环境条件下的安全性。深度学习已彻底改变了雷达信号处理，相比经典CFAR方法提供了更优越的检测能力。然而，这些模型的内存占用和高计算成本对其在资源受限的汽车边缘设备上的部署构成了重大障碍。本文对雷达张量处理网络的可行性和可移植性进行了全面研究。我们对各种主干机器学习架构进行了严格分析，并进行了代码剖析以识别处理瓶颈。此外，我们在NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin平台上对模型进行了基准测试。通过实施定制的优化流程——包括INT8量化、TensorRT编译和输入子采样——我们成功地将推理延迟降低至原来的1/6，实现了11.2 FPS（89毫秒）的帧率。这些结果定量地表明，通过硬件感知优化，基于深度学习的先进雷达感知对于实时ADAS应用是可行的。

## 提升自动驾驶中全天候自监督深度估计的鲁棒性 [Preprint]

原英文标题：Boosting Robustness for All-Weather Self-Supervised Depth Estimation in Autonomous Driving

作者：Mengshi Qi, Xiaoyang Bi, Xianlin Zhang, Huadong Ma

出版来源：arXiv

出版时间：2026-07-23

原英文摘要：Self-supervised depth estimation is challenging for safe autonomous driving under various adverse weather conditions due to sensor perception degradation. These challenges arise from two main aspects. Firstly, adverse conditions can distort pixel correspondences and violate the assumptions embedded in the self-supervised loss function, leading to erroneous depth predictions. Secondly, while radar is a widely adopted sensor in adverse weather conditions, the sparse distribution of radar points in the Point of View (POV) poses challenges for self-supervised fusion. To address these issues, we introduce a novel self-training pipeline using unpaired real all-weather data through multi-teacher distillation and robust radar fusion. We propose the Uncertainty-Aware Multi-Teacher Distillation method to generate diverse teacher models with different adverse condition inputs, and then employ uncertainty modeling to weigh the knowledge distillation loss. Additionally, we design the POV-BEV Radar Fusion approach, which leverages camera-pixel ray constraints to establish connections between the camera's Point of View (POV) and the radar's Bird's-Eye View (BEV). This approach enables the utilization of denser radar points, effectively capturing the complementary perspectives of both POV and BEV. Extensive quantitative and qualitative experiments demonstrate the robustness of our proposed method on all-weather datasets, achieving state-of-the-art performance. Our code and models are available at this https URL.

中文摘要：自监督深度估计在自动驾驶面临各种恶劣天气条件下具有挑战性，因为传感器感知会退化。这些挑战主要来自两个方面。首先，恶劣条件会扭曲像素对应关系，并违反自监督损失函数中的假设，导致错误的深度预测。其次，虽然雷达是恶劣天气条件下广泛采用的传感器，但雷达点在视角（POV）中的稀疏分布给自监督融合带来了挑战。为解决这些问题，我们引入了一种新的自训练流程，通过多教师蒸馏和鲁棒雷达融合来使用未配对的真实全天候数据。我们提出了不确定性感知多教师蒸馏方法，以不同的恶劣条件输入生成多样化的教师模型，然后采用不确定性建模来加权知识蒸馏损失。此外，我们设计了POV-BEV雷达融合方法，利用相机像素射线约束来建立相机视角（POV）与雷达鸟瞰图（BEV）之间的联系。这种方法能够利用更密集的雷达点，有效捕获POV和BEV的互补视角。大量定量和定性实验证明了我们提出的方法在全天候数据集上的鲁棒性，并达到了最先进的性能。我们的代码和模型可在以下网址获取：this https URL。

## CalibFusion：任务驱动外参优化的标定感知雷达-相机融合检测 [C]

原英文标题：CalibFusion: Task-Driven Extrinsic Refinement for Calibration-Aware Radar-Camera Fusion Detection

作者：Yuting Wan, Yuchen Lu, Liguo Sun, Jiuwu Hao, Zao Zhang, Pin Lv

出版来源：Advanced Intelligent Computing Technology and Applications (ICIC 2026), Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 16656

出版时间：2026-07-14

原英文摘要：Millimeter-wave (mmWave) Radar-Camera fusion is highly sensitive to extrinsic calibration, especially in weakly structured water-surface scenes where sparse Radar returns, clutter, and limited visual structure make reliable cross-modal correspondence unstable. Instead of treating calibration as a standalone geometric preprocessing step, we formulate Radar-Camera alignment as a detection-coupled latent refinement problem, where useful extrinsic correction is inferred from multimodal evidence and optimized according to downstream fusion quality. Based on this formulation, we propose CalibFusion, a calibration-aware fusion framework that embeds latent extrinsic refinement into the detection pipeline and feeds the refined geometry back into differentiable Radar feature generation for final detection. Experiments on WaterScenes, FLOW, and nuScenes show that CalibFusion consistently improves detection performance and robustness to synthetic extrinsic perturbations, achieving 67.7 mAP on WaterScenes and improving the strongest Radar-Camera baseline on the primary benchmark by 3.2 points. These results indicate that detection-coupled latent refinement provides an effective alternative to treating calibration as an isolated geometric objective in challenging water-surface perception.

中文摘要：毫米波雷达-相机融合对外参标定高度敏感，尤其是在弱结构化的水面场景中，稀疏的雷达回波、杂波和有限的视觉结构使得可靠的跨模态对应关系不稳定。我们没有将标定视为独立的几何预处理步骤，而是将雷达-相机对齐建模为与检测耦合的潜在优化问题，其中有效的外参修正从多模态证据中推断，并根据下游融合质量进行优化。基于这一公式，我们提出了CalibFusion，一个标定感知的融合框架，它将潜在外参优化嵌入检测流程，并将优化后的几何信息反馈到可微分的雷达特征生成中，用于最终检测。在WaterScenes、FLOW和nuScenes上的实验表明，CalibFusion持续提升了检测性能和对合成外参扰动的鲁棒性，在WaterScenes上达到67.7 mAP，并在主要基准上将最强的雷达-相机基线提升了3.2个百分点。这些结果表明，与将标定视为孤立几何目标相比，检测耦合的潜在优化为具有挑战性的水面感知提供了一种有效替代方案。

## 利用毫米波蜂窝基站与协方差感知多雷达融合的低空无人机监控 [J]

作者：Dhruva Anantha Datta, Sean Peters

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Radar Systems

出版时间：2026-07-22

原英文摘要：Low-altitude drones pose significant challenges for airspace safety and navigation, often requiring dedicated radar systems for monitoring. Commercial millimeter-wave cellular base stations provide dense urban infrastructure, large instantaneous bandwidths, directive antenna arrays, and software-defined processing chains that can support radar-like sensing. In particular, fifth-generation (5G) New Radio (NR) frequency range 2 (FR2) hardware provides bandwidths up to 400 MHz and directive massive multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) apertures that could enable sub-meter range resolution and multi-beam tracking using existing sites. Here, we investigate the feasibility of reusing commercial millimeter-wave cellular base stations as a distributed radar network for low-altitude uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) monitoring. We derive power signal-to-noise-ratio (SNR) bounds, quantify spatiotemporal resolutions, and discuss clutter and Doppler phenomenology for dense urban scenes. We develop a covariance-aware information-space fusion algorithm that combines bearing, elevation, and range observations from multiple stations. The proposed method rotates local measurement covariances into a global Cartesian frame and accumulates information using SNR gating, variance floors, and Huber reweighting for outlier suppression. Monte Carlo trials compare fused multi-radar positioning against the Global Positioning System (GPS) under both global navigation satellite system (GNSS)-available and GNSS-denied conditions. Our results show that, in typical line-of-sight (LoS) FR2 deployment settings, fused millimeter-wave cellular sensing can achieve error distributions competitive with the GPS comparator and can outperform it under GNSS-degraded conditions. We then outline a 5G NR-specific implementation path in which the baseband/field-programmable gate array (FPGA) stack schedules sensing bursts in time-division duplexing (TDD) idle slots and returns fused positions to cooperative UAVs.

## Radar Tracking Transformer：基于毫米波点云的隐私保护人体3D框与骨架跟踪 [J]

原英文标题：Radar Tracking Transformer for Privacy-Preserving Human 3D Box and Skeleton Tracking on mmWave Point Clouds

作者：Jae Yoon Jung, Jae Myung Shin, Ju Hyeon Kim, Deok Ho Yun, Kae Won Choi

出版来源：IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics

出版时间：2026-07-22

原英文摘要：Consumer electronics (CE) increasingly rely on ambient perception to deliver adaptive, safe, and privacy-preserving services. Unlike cameras, millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar can sense motion and coarse geometry in darkness and through visual occlusions while exposing far less appearance detail about users. This makes mmWave attractive for occupancy, intent, and interaction sensing in the home, including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) control, presence-aware media systems, and non-contact well-being features. We present Radar Tracking Transformer, an end-to-end framework that detects human 3D bounding boxes and normalized skeletons from radar point clouds and maintains identity-consistent tracks across frames. Specifically, each radar point is represented by normalized Cartesian coordinates, radial velocity, and echo power, which are embedded into transformer tokens by a shallow point-wise projection. Our design borrows the set-prediction principle of Detection Transformer (DETR) and the track query mechanism of recent video trackers, but adapts them to sparse, noisy radar point clouds through explicit inter-frame query propagation and track-aware matching. The provided implementation supports precision–recall (PR) curves and average precision (AP) for 3D boxes, percentage of correct keypoints (PCK) for skeleton accuracy, and tracking-specific metrics such as IDF1, higher order tracking accuracy (HOTA), and multiple object tracking accuracy (MOTA). On real mmWave radar data, the proposed method improves 3D box AP to 0.9333 (+0.0924 over a frame-only baseline), raises PCK@0.20 to 0.6278 (+0.0718), and outperforms VoteNet-based detector baselines, while running at 10 FPS, indicating suitability for interactive consumer devices.

## RADAR：以雷达为中心的锚定扩散与自适应校正用于鲁棒3D物体检测 [C]

原英文标题：RADAR: Radar-Centric Anchored Diffusion with Adaptive Rectification for Robust 3D Object Detection

作者：Xuan Zhang, Shuangjiao Zhai, Yujie Guo, Zichu Zhang, Xuanyu Zhang, Baojin Jing, Pinle Qin

出版来源：Advanced Intelligent Computing Technology and Applications (ICIC 2026), Lecture Notes in Computer Science, vol. 16661

出版时间：2026-07-16

原英文摘要：Reliable 3D object detection is fundamental to autonomous driving. Recent perception systems increasingly exploit complementary observations from multiple sensors to enhance scene understanding and robustness. However, in real world driving, images often remain available under low light, adverse weather, motion blur, or overexposure, while target-related visual evidence becomes degraded and only partially reliable. In this setting, discarding images loses residual semantic cues, whereas direct fusion can corrupt radar representations with misleading visual information. To address this challenge, a conditional diffusion refinement framework centered on radar representations is introduced for robust 3D object detection. The framework progressively refines radar bird’s eye view (BEV) features while treating degraded images as constrained auxiliary conditions. Specifically, the Physical Anchored Condition Space (PACS) extracts explicit radar physical properties to constrain visual participation. Conditioned on this space, the Hierarchical Diffusion Injection (HDI) mechanism injects radar properties and auxiliary conditions throughout denoising to generate candidate recovery states. The Reliability-Guided Residual Rectification (RGRR) module then regulates the write back of these corrections according to radar properties, occupancy support, and visual reliability. Experiments on VoD and TJ4DRadSet demonstrate consistent improvements over the radar-only baseline and competitive performance against recent 4D radar-based detectors, while degradation tests validate the robustness of RADAR under unreliable visual conditions.

中文摘要：可靠的3D物体检测是自动驾驶的基础。近年来的感知系统日益利用多传感器的互补观测来增强场景理解和鲁棒性。然而，在真实驾驶中，图像在低光、恶劣天气、运动模糊或过曝条件下往往仍然可用，但目标相关的视觉证据会退化，仅部分可靠。在这种情况下，丢弃图像会丢失残存的语义线索，而直接融合则可能用误导性的视觉信息破坏雷达表示。为应对这一挑战，提出了一种以雷达表示为中心的鲁棒3D物体检测条件扩散细化框架。该框架逐步细化雷达鸟瞰图（BEV）特征，同时将退化图像视为受限的辅助条件。具体而言，物理锚定条件空间（PACS）提取显式的雷达物理属性以约束视觉参与。在此条件下，分层扩散注入（HDI）机制在去噪过程中注入雷达属性和辅助条件，以生成候选恢复状态。随后，可靠性引导的残差校正（RGRR）模块根据雷达属性、占用支撑和视觉可靠性来调节这些校正的回写。在VoD和TJ4DRadSet上的实验表明，该方法相较于仅用雷达的基线有一致改进，并与近期的4D雷达检测器性能相当，同时退化测试验证了RADAR在不可靠视觉条件下的鲁棒性。

原英文摘要：The challenge of 3D multi-object tracking (3D MOT) is achieving robustness in real-world applications, for example under adverse conditions and maintaining consistency as distance increases. ## 恶劣水环境下自主水面车辆的鲁棒多模态鸟瞰图分割 [C]

原英文标题：Robust Multi-modal Bird's Eye View Segmentation for Autonomous Surface Vehicles in Challenging Water Environments

作者：Tao Wang, Yang Ping

出版来源：Advanced Intelligent Computing Technology and Applications (ICIC 2026), Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence, vol. 16666

出版时间：2026-07-15

原英文摘要：Autonomous Surface Vehicles (ASVs) are increasingly deployed for hazardous and time-consuming missions. Accurate and robust perception, particularly Bird’s Eye View (BEV) segmentation, is crucial for their safe navigation. While camera-based BEV segmentation has advanced significantly in road scenes, water environments present unique challenges such as severe light reflections, dynamic vessel swaying, and adverse weather conditions, which severely degrade monocular vision performance. Existing water surface BEV segmentation methods primarily rely on visual inputs, limiting their robustness. To address these limitations, we propose a novel multi-modal BEV segmentation framework that deeply integrates visual data with LiDAR and millimeter-wave radar information. Our approach leverages the complementary strengths of these sensors to overcome the inherent weaknesses of vision-only systems in challenging water environments.
